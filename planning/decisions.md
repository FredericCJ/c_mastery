# Accepted design decisions

- The owner selected independent study, Markdown plus a local website, mastery-first scope, 4–12-hour implementation projects, and detailed briefs/fixtures without starter repositories or solved project programs.
- The original prompt, expanded prompt and standalone taxonomy form one specification. The two fine-grained inventories match all 1,185 objective texts. Additional original-only requirements remain in a separate traceability register.
- The original T01–T35 IDs are coverage domains, not chronology. Contracts/ownership precede container design, and selected race/atomic/order foundations precede firmware sharing without requiring Linux pthread completion.
- T26 core coverage is awareness; advanced concurrent-object and kernel implementations are elective. Elective headings elsewhere remain explicit. Shared assessment evidence is permitted, but each objective has its own checklist item and shared effort is counted once.
- Library discovery is read-only. Current census/database pointers are pinned, returned historical paths are rebased only to verified actual payloads, and opaque/nonactive material is excluded. No library maintenance or reindexing is part of this task.
- The teaching content uses selected inspected passages and original explanations. Bibliographic verification, passage inspection and learner execution remain separate claims. Private source extracts and acquisition details are excluded from publication.
- The site uses Sphinx 8.2.3, MyST 4.0.1 and sphinx-book-theme 1.1.4 in a separate project-local environment. These installed compatible versions and their resolved dependencies are locked. Mathematical rendering assets are copied locally with their upstream license/provenance.
- Hosted teaching targets Linux x86-64 with GCC and Clang; physical labs target NUCLEO-F439ZI. QEMU MPS2 is a separately documented environment, not STM32 board emulation. Actual platform capabilities and source revisions are disclosed.
- 2026-10-12, fifth edition: the owner selected an expansion with eight new core domains (T57 Linux kernel programming, T58 RISC-V and AArch64 ports, T59 firmware lifecycle on F439/H745, T60 C-subset compiler, T61 verification and fuzzing, T62 advanced Linux I/O, T63 memory-safety offense and defense on the learner's own programs, T64 numerical and SIMD depth), no re-tiering, a physical-evidence campaign across all ten interfaces with the owned NUCLEO-F439ZI, NUCLEO-H745ZI-Q, SWD probe, logic analyzer, RS-232/422/485 and CAN/CAN FD transceivers and LAN8742A route, and fast-forwarding `main` to the accepted fourth edition before branching `overhaul/v5`. Generated outputs stay tracked; no remote or CI service is added.
- The fifth edition shall convey precise and idiomatic vocabulary for all domains: a glossary per domain with normative authorities, term links at first use, a vocabulary lint, vocabulary recall sets, a terminology criterion in the rubric, and a reviewed terminology pass over the existing 56 domains. Corrections to accepted prose are permitted only as logged hunks in `curriculum/v5/terminology_ledger.json` that reverse-apply to the accepted bytes; objective texts, project identities and budgets remain immutable.
- Glued word/number forms and doubled list markers in accepted text are repaired at render time by `cmastery.text.normalize_prose`, never by rewriting pinned bytes; the publication lints reject any rendered form the normalizer would repair.
