The three communication projects are families of 4–12-hour milestones. Reused artifacts retain their original project IDs and hours; a family view must not add another integration budget. Simulated byte/frame adapters test higher layers but cannot satisfy electrical/PHY evidence. The seven-layer protocol is an original teaching design, not an ISO protocol implementation. Internet protocols use their own normative contracts and do not match seven OSI layers one to one.

Primary physical platforms remain the existing Linux/STM32F439 route. F439 classic CAN does not provide CAN FD; a supported external controller or alternate MCU is a separate hardware route. A supplied transceiver or PHY implements analog signaling. Learners implement and verify digital driver, framing and higher-layer behavior, and measure the physical boundary.
