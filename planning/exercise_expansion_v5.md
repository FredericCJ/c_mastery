# Evidence-based exercise expansion

The owner selected foundations first on 2026-10-16 and then authorized implementation.
The whole-curriculum scope remains comprehensive. The existing 4,879-hour catalog
is historical accounting, not a ceiling; additional exercises carry separate
uncalibrated effort records. No accepted objective, unit or project budget is rewritten.

B01 added eight mechanism-driven exercises in a sidecar catalog. Their identifiers
are X-T02-001, X-T04-001, X-T03-001 through X-T03-005 and X-T35-001. The family
`foundation_depth` has no old-project graph entries or old-unit budget ownership.
Existing artifact contracts remain the prerequisite vocabulary; learner sources
are imported explicitly and adapters disclose their limits.

Canonical briefs contain fenced metadata and original learner-facing teaching.
The generated exercise catalog drives CLI initialization, checks, study sheets
and separate exercise evidence. Assessor implementations, analyses and withheld
variants remain in the assessor tree. Public tests do not certify reasoning or
independent transfer. The common rubric still requires all six dimensions at least
2 and at least two at 3, with noncompensable correctness/ownership/dependency/failure criteria.

The extension registers no new taxonomy leaf in B01: the selected deficiencies
are depth gaps within existing objectives. The allocated T57–T76 domain recipes
and research taxonomies now contain twenty new domains, 178 projects and
653 leaf objectives. Further omissions discovered in the accessible corpus still
require explicit additive taxonomy records and justified prerequisites.

The modern implementation order is corrected to require T70 small-buffer storage
before T69.05. T65 initially records direct storage and operation counts; allocation
comparisons using T66's counting allocator are a later integration obligation,
not a fictional prerequisite already available to the pilot.

`curriculum/v5/expansion/remaining_work.json` is the canonical continuation map;
`gaps.json` records unresolved evidence and research. `coverage_depth.json`,
`domain_progress.json` and `modern_discovery.json` are generated by
`tools/audit_expansion_v5.py`. A missing named test alone does not establish
inadequate analysis coverage. An existing link alone does not establish sufficient depth.

B08 closes an integration-audit omission: the accepted catalog also contains
twelve checkpoint projects and eight Linux/MCU capstone projects outside the
T01–T56 numbering scheme. Their supplements assess cross-component contracts,
failure and recovery, target assumptions and delayed transfer. Completion tracks
all 429 retained projects as well as all 1,881 leaf objectives; covering domain
leaves alone cannot close the integration work.

Current batch receipts live separately from every pre-expansion review record.
Historical receipt hashes are pinned before implementation; publication tooling
preserves those records. Reports distinguish authored, executable, executed,
reviewed, physically observed and learner-assessed claims. No hardware flashing,
host security-setting changes or external targets are authorized by this work.
