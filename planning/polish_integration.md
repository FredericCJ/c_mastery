## Reading the evidence

- The [PMU capture and verification guide](../reviews/v5/expansion/main_integration/pmu/README.md) distinguishes the current Intel campaign from the three archived AMD observations. Each dossier's original build manifest records its executable hash. The old branch contains the measured disassembly, source hashes, event definitions and rows; it omits those three original executable files. The archived at-capture runner hash also differs from the handoff's final runner.
- The [NUCLEO session](../reviews/v5/expansion/main_integration/physical/README.md) identifies the connected board, the image it executed, the serial output captured and the restoration checks. Emulator observations and historical board records have their own provenance.
- The new inspection exercises connect compilation, ELF structures, raw bytes, disassembly, a debugger, Ghidra and the Cortex-M4 image through runnable artifacts. Their evidence is keyed to the locally built binaries and tool versions.
- Omitted style-stage receipts, unavailable original binaries and prior-host observations supply no additional current-host or independent-learner credit. A new binary may generate different addresses, register choices and timings.
