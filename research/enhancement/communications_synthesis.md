The strongest change in the design is to separate component responsibility from a familiar protocol name. A UART or synchronous USART peripheral supplies a character/clocking mechanism; a MAX3232 or RS-485 transceiver supplies voltage conversion or differential drivers; a byte-channel contract supplies explicit local buffering and failures; an application supplies records and addressing.
