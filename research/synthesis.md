# Research synthesis and authoring gate

The research sequence was broad discovery, explicit reflection, focused passage inspection and synthesis before teaching authorship. Three independent domain investigations searched the local Knowledge metadata catalog and the union of SWE graph collections, then inspected actual held book passages and primary web documents. The graph supplied leads, never substitutes for document content. The library and its manifests were read only.

The public registry contains bounded resource records. A work may have two independently inspected records with different locator scopes; record count is not unique publication count. The ledger preserves those scopes rather than silently broadening an assignment to an entire book. Six language textbook editions, the systems textbook editions and the embedded/testing editions have actual selected-passage evidence. Historical manuscripts, standards drafts, current contracts and implementation source have distinct teaching roles.

## Reflection that changed the design

The first search exposed a catalog limitation: conjunctive metadata retrieval and limited enrichment can miss a held work. Focused title/author queries and actual edition checks replaced absence claims. The SWE union includes edgeless works that a traversal alone would omit; its historical verification label is not chapter or edition verification.

Source comparisons corrected important ambiguities. The held CSAPP third edition is the Global Edition, with different page numbering. WG14's accessible drafts are not identical to published ISO editions. The inspected STM32 reference is Rev21; F439 RCC belongs to chapter 6 rather than the older device group's chapter 7. The board manual capture is Rev8, while the current portal identifies a later revision. Kernel memory-model rules, ISO C atomics, MMIO ordering and DMA ownership must stay separate.

Historical K&R/Hanson allocator and pointer examples include assumptions unsuitable for unquestioned modern reuse. They become critique and modernization exercises. A libc call that looks allocation-free may have transitive costs; the component profile therefore requires object and binary audits plus qualified allocation evidence. Numeric and formal-method readings expose model boundaries: mathematical reals are not IEEE floating-point execution, bounded unwinding is not an unbounded theorem, and asynchronous interrupts require explicit environmental treatment.

## Accepted teaching spine

Modern C and Effective C supply selected language explanations, complemented by inspected WG14 and compiler material. Hanson, CLRS, Higham and foundational design/correctness papers motivate representation, algorithms, error analysis and contracts. CSAPP Global Edition and TLPI connect C to linking and Linux interfaces, checked against versioned manuals, POSIX and selected implementation source. Arm/ST primary documents govern core, device and board questions; White and Grenning supply bounded explanatory and testing supplements. Hoare, Parnas, Dijkstra, Wilson, Lamport, Boehm–Adve, Liu–Layland and Roofline supply foundational arguments with their original assumptions retained.

The exact assignment locator must exist in the registry and lie inside the recorded inspection. New passage assignments require a further focused inspection. A selected reading can illuminate a mechanism without covering every leaf objective; original teaching and a concrete project still carry that obligation.

## Nonblocking gaps and boundaries

Levine, Yiu, the inaccessible CERT route and the original Boehm 2005 paper remain unassignable leads. Their discovery does not block the supported core; no required reading uses them. Modern memory reclamation and deep kernel work remain elective, with further evidence required for implementation claims. No full ISO conformance audit, hardware experiment, installed QEMU probe, complete peripheral chapter reading, WCET proof or formal-tool execution was performed during research.

The seven executed GCC probes are separate bounded empirical evidence. They check selected C17/C23 snippets on the recorded WSL toolchain, without certifying Clang, a whole language edition, bare-metal support or every libc facility.

## Gate decision

Accepted for authorship on 2026-10-04. All domains have a usable evidence base, all investigations contain discovery/reflection/focused search, provenance and edition corrections are recorded, and remaining limitations can be exposed in module contracts. This accepts the research basis, not the finished curriculum. Teaching quality, fixture oracles, exact traceability, workload accounting and publication behavior require their own reviews.
