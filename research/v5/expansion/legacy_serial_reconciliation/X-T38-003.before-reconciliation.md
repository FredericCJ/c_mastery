## Problem

Implement sd_sync. A5 is LSB-first. LBCL0 yields14 edges and LBCL1 yields16; CPOL sets idle level and CPHA chooses odd/even capture edge. changing1 with TE or RE set returns BUSY unchanged. changing1 with both disabled succeeds with empty trace. A transfer with TE0 is invalid even when RE1. Produce per-edge clock/sample tables and the omitted last-bit pulse.
