# Parser source inspection

Thompson1968 was read in its four-page original PDF, particularly compilation/current-next-list narrative and printed p422 notes on nullable repetition and duplicate runtime entries. Cox's original2007 article was read for syntax/backreference distinction and addstate/startlist/step. The bounded typed-state implementation is original; it does not reproduce Thompson machine code or adopt an unbounded regex feature set.

Gottlieb's author-hosted lecture6 closure/goto/canonical-collection definitions and lecture7 LR behavior/table construction/conflict passages were read directly. Captured course pages are undated;2026 is their retrieval identity, not a claimed publication date. The construction here is SLR. Conflicts are not automatically grammar ambiguity or evidence against all LR methods.

Pratt's original scanned paper printed pp47–48 was rendered and visually read, confirming the rbp<lbp continuation, nud/led distinction and stated parser properties. The remastered HTML section3 was also consulted; source hash binds the original PDF. Ford's author-hosted2004 paper section2 operators/predicates and section2.1 unified lexical syntax were read. Whole-paper proofs were not claimed inspected. Nonfinal PENDING, capture rollback and the forward-only bounded DAG are explicit original adaptations.

Private captures are under ignored build/corpus_parser_sources. Resource records preserve exact byte hashes and locator scope; no raw copyrighted extract is published. Search results and SWE element descriptions served only as discovery leads. Actual retained parser_follow.c was inspected for the distinct packrat/cache/seed-growth mechanism before deciding not to duplicate it.
