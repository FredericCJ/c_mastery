# T18–T22 depth audit and implemented expansion

The original 20 projects and their 150 objectives already teach contracts,
architecture, documentation, error handling and production-library practice.
The expansion adds an explicit assessment for every leaf, preserving original
IDs, hours, teaching, fixtures and accepted receipts. Canonical supplements are
`curriculum/v5/expansion/legacy_assessments/PR-T18-*.json` through `PR-T22-*.json`;
solutions are separately published under `assessor/legacy_depth/`.

The audit distinguished nominal fixture behavior from ownership, atomic failure,
reentrancy and independent transfer. Added criteria require complete overlap
contracts, mutable/borrowed view invalidation, callback removal and destruction
during dispatch, interface versioning, source-bound examples, error preservation,
partial acquisition rollback, observability costs and reproducible packaging.
Each leaf maps to its concrete artifact, expected relation, 0–3 rubric, graduated
hints and delayed changed-condition task. Existing taxonomy leaves are refined,
not renamed or replaced by a disconnected abstraction survey.

`labs/api_depth` supplies two additional executable anchors: all-overlap-rejecting
bounded append and a four-slot generation-aware callback registry with deferred
removal/destruction and reentry behavior. Its campaign and experiment receipts
record actual compiler/control/oracle and strict-link observations. Their narrow
scope does not establish every criterion in these domains or target execution.

`resources.json` records actual bounded local and primary passages. Exact C17 and
the listed books not freshly inspected remain explicit reference gaps; a title,
bibliography or previous source register is not counted as a fresh inspection.

Run `.venv/Scripts/python.exe research/v5/expansion/legacy_diagnostics/validate.py --scope api`.
The recorded run passed 20 project contracts, 150 leaf criteria, 20 finite fixture
comparisons and 10 negative controls. The validator does not rewrite historical
receipts or infer learner, independent-review or physical evidence. See
`validation.json` for exact inputs/results and `remaining_work.json` for required
criterion-level execution and delayed assessment obligations.
