# Why these are required additions

The original PR-T14-09 choose-one advanced structure and PR-T15-10 specialized
algorithm headings do not establish these mechanisms. Actual retained PR-T15-10
criteria/implementation studied run-length framing, and the inspected prior
advanced-structure work does not implement these three range indexes. The SWE
design-element records identify distinct recurring mechanisms: fenwick-tree,
segment-tree, interval-tree and suffix-array. Discovery prompted primary reading;
the metadata entries themselves are not source-inspection evidence.

The added exercises have different necessary boundaries: monotone frequency
selection and rounding; noncommutative aggregation and map composition; rotation
augmentation and endpoint semantics; and rank refinement, prefix equality and
nonoverlap. They share packaging and admission conventions, not mechanical
exercise quotas. Their altered assumptions are implemented through explicit
policies and assessed independently after the recorded delay.

T14.14.b and T14.15.a/b are the precise retained objective mappings. The more
specific B-tree/skip-list/filter objective and persistence/probability objective
remain their own obligations. T15.15.a/b/c support suffix construction, measured
workload selection and an independent oracle. Historical identities, hours,
accepted evidence and existing mechanisms remain untouched.

Corpus relevance screening continues beyond the prior taxonomy and100-element
modern census. The metadata map records categories, named mechanism candidates,
confirmed additions and unresolved candidate checks separately. A search hit or
category-to-domain association is not teaching, implementation or assessment
coverage. Likewise, these four packages do not automatically close the full
discovery obligation or all variants mentioned by a source index.
