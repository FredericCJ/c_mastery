# T39 service/frame/bridge completion audit

Inspected the actual PR-T39-01/02/03 briefs, twelve canonical criterion/V1
pairs, the complete retained Ethernet contract and its concrete frame/bridge
implementation, plus current T41 driver/HTTP public contracts. The ordinary
wrapper, untagged frame, CRC and finite bridge mechanisms already existed.
Changed conditions lacked an executable record adapter, separate tagged-frame
contract and explicit eviction/loop controls. Those are the new implementations;
the retained providers remain byte-identical.

Original records are preserved by pure additive attachment. No accepted unit,
hours, ID or historical receipt is rewritten. A board-route analysis is not
claimed from a byte-codec test: the independent objective mapping retains its
source argument and required physical ledger separately. T39-08's actual MCU
consumer and drift/route work, all T40 and mixed LRUN23/24 parents remain required
after this package. Existing X-T39 sidecar variants require their own exact-ID
reconciliation; this package does not silently assign them a pass.

Read primary passages: IEEE workshop2011 slides5–7,9–10,14 textual material;
P802.1ad/D2.4 (2004, unapproved draft)8.7 and9.1–9.6; RFC894 Frame Format;
RFC1122 §1.1.3; RFC9293 §2.2/3.7; RFC9110 §3.3; LAN8742A Rev1.1 §3.2–3.2.4;
UM1974 Rev8 §6.11/Table11 and Rev11 §7.11/Table11. Only the indicated selected
passages are claimed, not complete books/standards or an independent review.
Local PDF text extraction was read; workshop screenshot responses did not
expose usable images here and are not counted as visual diagram inspection.

The read-only held-library schema4 catalog was pinned and queried for802.1Q
(no matches) and802.3 (two secondary publications, no exact standard found).
No excluded `_old` tree or opaque package was traversed. Search results are
discovery evidence, not content inspection. The exact current normative
IEEE802.1Q/802.3 bodies remain a source gap; the current IEEE landing page is
not credited as a read standard. The new restricted historical CFI=0 profile
does not claim current VLAN/DEI conformance. Strict first-principles work uses
only the original C providers; host Python/zlib are independent verification.

Native and Cortex-M observations are author evidence only. Physical board,
PHY/cable/magnetics, actual wire FCS/duplex and timing remain unobserved;
external review and independent delayed learner assessment remain separate.
