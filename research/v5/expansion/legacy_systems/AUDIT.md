# T07–T11 depth expansion

The accepted projects contain substantive construction contracts. The expansion retains those contracts, their C17 baseline and dependency profiles, accepted identities, hours and historical evidence. It adds 162 explicit leaf assessments across 24 projects, each with a concrete task, causal misconception, evidence criterion, graduated topic hints, level0–3 rubric and a changed-condition assessment delayed at least seven days. Separate assessor analyses remain under `assessor/legacy_depth/`; they are not learner implementations.

The audit read the relevant module teaching passages, all project contracts/steps/objective checklists, all 24 synthetic fixtures, the applicable oracle branches, the foundations negative compiler cases, the machine-image laboratory contracts, runtime tests and artifact producer. Unit objective/test links and selected operation steps were inspected; this is not a claim to have re-reviewed every historical learner portfolio or every source passage assigned by the old bibliography.

## Consequential findings and response

- T07 has real diagnostic compilation controls but its general fixtures model closure, cycles, search policy or budgets. Those models do not execute a build graph, verify package relocation or demonstrate reproducibility. The 36 new criteria distinguish stage provenance, stale intermediates, generated-output failures, target-local requirements, host/target tools, acquisition identity, negative-test failure causes and whole-file versus semantic reproducibility.
- T08 retains real weighted-sum and hostile-caller tests. They do not establish aggregate return conventions, every frame/unwind obligation, Arm floating-point interoperability, inline-assembly constraints or compiler/CPU ordering equivalence. The 30 criteria assess those mechanisms explicitly without claiming x86 observations validate Arm or privileged environments.
- T09 retains real archive, relocation, TLS/version, budget and image gates. The 36 criteria add independently checked ELF extents, unresolved-set traces, signed relocation overflow, archive extraction versus KEEP, loader lifetime/binding, VMA/LMA/file accounting and ABI semantic compatibility. The existing synthetic checksum is not a complete image-format implementation.
- T10 retains an actual no-libc entry and exact-byte syscall gate. The 30 criteria assess bounded environment/auxv decoding, stack capture before alignment, runtime initialization ownership, cleanup distinctions, synthetic partial/EINTR/zero-progress transitions, static dependency origins and controlled before-main diagnosis. The ordinary 4096-byte output test does not force every short-write or cancellation path.
- T11 retains byte primitives, wide division and dependency-audit gates. The 30 criteria deepen compiler-helper ABI contracts, the129-bit intermediate argument in128-bit division, atomic/instrumentation dependencies, all valid16-byte arena partitions, explicit refusing services and statically resolved forbidden-helper detection. A final empty undefined-symbol report is insufficient for the latter claim.

The family selection is mechanism-driven: reasoning, construction where required, diagnosis, verification and changed assumptions dominate these toolchain topics. Delayed independent transfer applies to every explicit leaf assessment. These are not additional executable-unit counts. An analysis requirement does not become executed evidence because it has a rubric.
