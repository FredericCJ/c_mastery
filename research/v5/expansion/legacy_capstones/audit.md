## Actual checks

`.conda/python.exe assessor/legacy_depth/verify_capstone_boundaries.py` passed 68 two-chunk framing cases, corrupt-then-valid resynchronization, six finite boundary groups and six deliberate faulty-model controls. The report is `reviews/v5/expansion/legacy_capstones/boundary_oracles.json`. It is host mathematical/model evidence, not a compiled C service or firmware campaign. The preserved initial failure detected a wrong authored RAM sum; corrected totals are 1896 bytes, 2200 remaining under 4096, and 2056 after adding a 160-byte exception frame, exceeding 2048 by eight.

`assessor/legacy_depth/validate_capstone_scope.py` validates all eight supplements against the current shared checker and records exact source hashes. Its report distinguishes structural/source-binding success from execution, independent assessment and physical validation.
