# T28.09 depth reconciliation

The existing FW ADC anchor masks a supplied DR and distinguishes EOC/timeout; it does not establish stabilization, sequencing, trigger loss, analog settling, DAC or comparator support. This successor keeps that actual provider as a65,536-word comparison and adds distinct criterion support. Original criteria, seven-day variants, IDs, historical binding and hours remain unchanged.

Ordinary and changed acquisition now include exclusive common-clock ownership, timestamp-after-enable stabilization, two-channel full-batch publication, late overrun, timeout/cancellation, generation retirement and a declared trigger/service event model. The actual F439 register adapter and TIM3 provider are linked under strict profile E. Analog support derives RC settling, checks finite configurations against independent rational oracles and separates reference/source/calibration errors. The supported-feature ledger distinguishes F439 DAC from G474 comparator and includes silicon-revision errata. Original helpers are audited at object and linked-image levels.

The initial pilot is retained as failed evidence: equivalent overrun control, omitted whole-clock-tree premise in the oracle, PPC64 stdio/import errors and copied linker entry were corrected before the current campaign. Clang's real jump-table and LR-based register addressing required decoder extensions; no required address was removed. Capture validation rejects incomplete premises but cannot attest provenance. No hardware has been operated. Full RM0440 and the exact F439 PA4 header route remain source-access dependencies. T28.10–.12 and T29 are still required implementation, not external blockers.
