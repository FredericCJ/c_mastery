# T46.10 depth audit

The retained runtime.host test was a partial anchor. The three original ordinary
and delayed criteria require rights, actual fixed four-byte storage, format
migration, two different uncertainty cases and a new increment receipt protocol.
This package imports actual unchanged providers and implements those separate
mechanisms. No old acceptance receipt or criterion is rewritten. Families B/C/D/F/J
use actual dependency composition, controlled local violations, independent
protocol histories, generated code/resource audits and changed assumptions.

The provider uses full-page redo and37write/flush boundaries. The supplied medium
models discard/persist-all reply loss, not arbitrary device faults. The explicit
SSRAM4M emulator profile is necessary for its two copied medium snapshots; it does
not establish baseline64KiB feasibility. Published ISO, physical observations,
independent review and delayed learner evidence remain separate. Completion
requires source-stable author campaigns plus exact export/negative guards.
