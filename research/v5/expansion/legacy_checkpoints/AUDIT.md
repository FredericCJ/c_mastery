The12 accepted checkpoints retain their identities, hours, profiles, prerequisite dossiers and implementation obligations. The accepted project graph and family artifact imports were read directly; a title or graph edge alone is not implementation evidence.

The current anchors are deliberately narrower than the checkpoint briefs:

- P1's accepted-prefix record/queue handoff does not establish arena/pool storage legality, alignment or allocator failure cleanup.
- P2's retained active-byte writer adapter does not establish actual Linux entry, ABI, ELF mapping or syscall error translation.
- P3's `in_stream` consumes one-byte lengths and decimal text, has a31-byte payload limit and enters a terminal failed state. It is not the shared A5/version/sequence/length<=32/CRC framing and resynchronization protocol. Both remain named, with no promotion of the smaller anchor.
- P4 tests a serialized DMA-generation/quiescence adapter. It does not establish F439 reset, physical UART/DMA behavior, actual interrupt ordering or timing.
- P5 tests synchronous cancellation/completion ordering and exactly-once release. It does not establish a concurrent reader/decoder/worker service, descriptor lifetime or shutdown under load.
- P6 tests a fixed integer filter and synthetic measurement capture. It does not establish the rational FIR contract, vector dispatch, numerical equivalence or physical speedup.

The graph's prerequisite rows omit guide-required P3 from P4 and P1/P3 from P5. The additive contracts explicitly require those learner artifacts without changing historical graph rows.
