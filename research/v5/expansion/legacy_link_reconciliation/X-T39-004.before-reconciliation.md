## Problem

Implement ed_bridge for three ports, four entries and TTL10. Learn A on1 at0, B on2 at1; B-to-A forwards mask1, A-to-A filters mask0. At exactly age10 expire A before lookup. Fill all four entries, attempt a fifth source and prove FULL leaves table/time/mask unchanged. Reject group source and regressing time.

## Necessity

Expiration order and its equality boundary affect forwarding. Updating time or expiring entries before a failed full-table insertion violates the atomic failure contract. A same-ingress hit must not become an unknown flood.

## Reasoning

Expiration order and its equality boundary affect forwarding. Updating time or expiring entries before a failed full-table insertion violates the atomic failure contract. Explain which guarantees are runtime checked and which require programmer discipline or external verification.
