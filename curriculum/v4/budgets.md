# Workload conservation and a sustainable rhythm

Keep the full catalog at 4,879 planned hours and the conservative core at 4,799.
Passing the entry diagnostic without its remaining remediation retains the
existing 4,777-hour personal-plan estimate. Time estimates remain provisional.

Every working unit owns an exact number of minutes within one original project.
All units together retain 3,599 implementation/verification hours. The 56 new
executable units replace or strengthen work already assigned in those envelopes.
Their tests and new family directories do not add another budget.

Across modules and integrations, 593 hours remain for reading, 368 for assessment
and 319 for consolidation. The conservation ledger assigns 160 assessment hours
formerly used for retrospective alternatives/dossier prose to prior design
comparisons and milestone reviews. It assigns 261.25 consolidation hours formerly
used for manual delayed reconstruction to 627 sets with five planned five-minute
reviews. The remaining 57.75 consolidation hours cover longer reconstructions and
other consolidation. The 32 integration assessment/consolidation hours are part
of these totals, not another allocation.

The integration slice remains 208 hours: 160 implementation/verification plus
48 reading/assessment/consolidation. Importing an existing codec, queue or VM pays
only for the new adapter and changed-boundary evidence. Earlier implementation is
charged once to its producer.

For a ten-hour week, the existing approximate split of two hours reading and
prediction, five implementation, two verification and one assessment/retrieval
remains useful. Short reviews occupy that last category; they are not added on
top. Use actual category budgets when a project requires more verification.
Twenty-hour weeks can complete more units, but delayed work still occurs on
separate days. Remediation and equipment waiting are recorded separately.

The session queue is a recommendation rather than a minimum daily debt. Record
active minutes and which activity a longer review replaces. A full six-score
review on every small unit would silently inflate the budget; perform it at the
reusable component or project boundary instead.
