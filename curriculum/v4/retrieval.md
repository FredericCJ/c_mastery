# Recall, spacing and transfer during real project work

There is one scheduled set per micro-topic, with mechanism, trace/implementation
and changed-context variants. Projects reference those sets rather than creating
duplicate queues. The 627 sets cover the 1,881 objective facets, but each attempt
may score only what the response actually demonstrates.

## Attempt before feedback

Write the first prediction, draw a state/ownership diagram, trace a short input,
or reconstruct a small operation before looking at the old implementation. Record
assistance. After sealing the answer, inspect feedback and locate the first broken
assumption. A corrected answer is valuable practice; it is not a delayed test of
retention when produced in the same session.

Use the existing scale: zero is absent or materially wrong; one is partial or
cued; two is independently correct with the relevant boundary; three demonstrates
transfer under a changed condition with justified reasoning. A passing executable
test cannot grade the explanation or prove independent work.

## A bounded queue

Review approximately two due sets in ten to fifteen minutes at the start of an
actual study session. Prefer unresolved failures and prerequisite contracts, then
older material. The initial successful-review gaps are 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days
from the relevant preceding event. They are configurable engineering defaults.
Unstarted and elective material does not become due merely because it exists.

After the initial sequence, later consumption and cumulative milestones supply
maintenance practice. A missed session creates one overdue opportunity, not a
debt for every missed day. After a long gap, resume with a bounded diagnostic;
elapsed time alone is neither proof of forgetting nor reason to delete evidence.

At a component handoff, deduplicate the critical requirements of the imported
contracts. Each needs an unaided score of at least two in the relevant context
within fourteen days or a fresh preflight demonstration. This freshness window
is a policy, not a biological constant. Split a large preflight across sessions.
Do not quietly sample away a required ownership or synchronization assumption.

A failure reopens the affected objective/claim, supplies targeted corrective
practice and schedules a later independent attempt. Other study stays available.
Physical observations, dependency audits and implementation evidence retain their
own gates. The ledger keeps original and superseding evaluations.

## Useful interleaving

Compare already-studied mechanisms whose selection matters: semaphore versus
mutex versus notification; stack exhaustion versus corruption; asymptotic growth
versus measured cycles; ordinary accesses versus acquire/release publication.
Choose the mechanism before executing it. Interleaving is not random interruption
while the learner is still acquiring the first example.

Use changed-condition reconstruction already budgeted for assessment: capacity
8 to 7, new HTTP segmentation, a different allocator lifetime, an API invoked
from an ISR, or a peer reset during ownership transfer. Larger reconstruction
tasks are scheduled units, not invisible homework after the session.

## Evidence behind the design

[Karpicke and Blunt (2011)](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2011/2011_Karpicke_Blunt_Science.pdf)
studied conceptual retrieval with science texts. Applying reconstruction to C
mechanisms is a curriculum design inference.
[Karpicke and Bauernschmidt (2011)](https://learninglab.psych.purdue.edu/downloads/2011/2011_Karpicke_Bauernschmidt_JEPLMC.pdf)
support spacing while finding no inherently superior relative interval pattern
in their experiment. The selected schedule does not claim universal optimality.
[YeckehZaare and Resnick (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-025-00322-5)
examined incentives to practice on separate days. These publications motivate
practice design; they do not establish this curriculum's effectiveness or duration.
