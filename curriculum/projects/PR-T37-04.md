## Contract

Build a small original stage-schedule model/checker for declared IF/ID/EX/MEM/WB, single issue, in order. ALU result is available after EX; load after MEM; forwarded values enter next-cycle EX; register-file writes occur before reads in a cycle. A taken branch resolves at EX end, squashes younger IF/ID and refetches target next cycle. Do not label this a physical Cortex-M4/x86 pipeline.
