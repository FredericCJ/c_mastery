## Inputs and expected results

Trace 0, 16, 0, 64, 0, 16, 80, 16, 0 yields six misses: four compulsory, two conflict, zero capacity. Trace 0, 16, 32, 48, 64, 0 ends with a capacity miss. Hit 1 ns/additional penalty 30 ns gives AMAT 21 ns for the first trace. Declared 100 instructions, CPI 1.2, 24 references, rate 0.25, penalty 20 cycles gives 240 cycles total and 120 ns at 2 GHz.

## Steps

## Failure cases

Reject negative/invalid addresses or zero/non-power-of-two geometry in the general wrapper. Include cold start, repeated hits, two conflicting blocks, more than four distinct blocks and an invalid uninitialized-tag mutation. Miss penalty is additional to hit cost under this contract; a total-miss-latency input must be converted explicitly.

## Hints and recovery

A repeated block can miss by conflict while the equal-capacity shadow still holds it. The three-C category is evaluated against shadow state before servicing that reference. Normalize addresses to declared blocks; do not confuse array element count with byte offset. Check cycles/seconds conversion before interpreting a large time.
