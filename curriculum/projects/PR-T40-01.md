## Contract

Produce the executable design boundary for EDU7-v1 before implementing packets. Use the fixed [original wire contract](../../research/enhancement/networking_contracts.json), rather than choosing new addresses or retry policies. A=17, R=34 and B=51 use two independent segments. R terminates L1/L2 and forwards L3 without decoding presentation/session data. Map application, typed representation, dialogue, bounded reliable message, routed datagram, local frame and physical symbols to their actual responsibility.

Define each layer's submit/receive/tick/completion interface, result and ownership. A synchronous receive span is immutable and borrowed until callback return. Waiting layers copy into bounded private storage. An asynchronous accepted transmit span remains immutable until its specified completion; BUSY consumes nothing. The ledger must identify which completions permit buffer reuse and which establish peer delivery.

## Inputs and expected results

The supplied fixture starts with a conservative L6 service capacity of 80 octets, L5 header 7, L4 payload 24/header 8, L3 header 6 and L2 header 5/CRC 2. The capacity proof gives session storage 87 and fragment capacities 24, 24, 24, 15. Schema 1 cannot actually encode 80 legal octets: its largest canonical record is 1+5*2+(1+2+2+1+16)=33, giving session SDU 40 and fragments 24, 16. The fixture recomputes both bounds. The largest generated network packet is 38 octets and link body 45; generic allowed link payload 40 separately requires body 47 and worst escaped frame 96. Treat storage capacity and accepted syntax as different bounds.

Output a boundary table, nested worked trace and capacity assertions. The fixture's independent arithmetic verifier derives all sizes; your C tests demonstrate that capacities and checked arithmetic enforce them.

## Steps

1. Draw A→R→B and attach three namespaces: local link 1/2, node 17/34/51, ports 7/8.

2. Write a state/lifetime contract for every interface, including upper-delivery rejection and lower BUSY.

3. Derive lengths outward from the 80-octet capacity envelope and the actual 33-octet canonical maximum, then inward from the 40-octet link limit; reconcile all three bounds.

4. Instrument a host pipeline with layer events without yet solving the protocols. Poison borrowed input after return and attempt a forbidden queued reference.

5. Review the trace against X.200 purposes and Peterson/Davie's encapsulation passage. Mark original protocol decisions separately from reference-model definitions.

## Failure cases

Reject an 81-octet presentation record before publication, arithmetic beyond configured capacity, a retained pointer to a borrowed span, and treating R as an application endpoint. A local CRC success may not be reported as session/application success. A BUSY return may not advance or free the sender's buffer.
