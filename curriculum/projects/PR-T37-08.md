## Inputs and expected results

Original equation inputs 1.2 million instructions, CPI 1.5, 2 GHz give 0.9 ms. Accelerating 60% threefold gives 0.54 ms, speedup 5/3, limit 2.5. Synthetic sample medians 100/95 ns give ratio 20/19; ranges 96..110 and 93..120 include outliers. These samples demonstrate reporting only and must not be presented as measured hardware performance.

## Steps

Verify every timed result/checksum outside the timing region. Record exact source/binary, CPU/ABI/compiler/options, input sizes/order, clock resolution/overhead and warm/cold policy. Alternate condition order, retain all raw samples and record migration/frequency/interrupt controls or their absence. Report median/range and original operation counts; explain whether timer granularity or setup dominates. Compare the same outputs with the declared equations but do not force measured timings to equal an assumed CPI. Thumb emulator runs may provide ISA correctness only; use Linux x86 native execution for required measurements if no physical MCU is available.

## Failure cases

Reject mismatched outputs, optimized-away work, uncontrolled changing inputs, unit confusion, dropped outliers and an unsupported energy/WCET conclusion. A timer interval below its usable resolution is inconclusive; enlarge repetitions within a batch with correctness retained and disclose the normalization. Do not blindly subtract overhead to produce negative times.

## Acceptance and evidence

For .10a submit dimensionally checked equations and Amdahl derivation; .10b reproducible native experiment, identical results and at least 31 samples/condition; .10c raw distribution/confounders, work-versus-time table and explicit limits. No speedup threshold is required; a slower correct assembly kernel can satisfy the project with a sound explanation.

Keep the evidence items separate for each linked topic's three objectives. Retain the actual input/output replay, build identity, dependency/storage ledger, first counterexample and limits. Deterministic synthetic expectations do not substitute for required executable or device observations.

## Hints and recovery

The improved fraction is a fraction of baseline elapsed work, not source lines. Average CPI already aggregates the measured instruction mix. Keep raw timings even if a median is convenient. If measurements wobble, first check order/cache/clock granularity before rewriting assembly.

## Transfer challenge

Repeat one input family at a different size and predict whether algorithmic work or fixed overhead dominates. Treat any trend as conditional evidence rather than a universal performance ranking.
