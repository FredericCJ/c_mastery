## Contract

## Steps

- **T62.05:** Cancel and target have distinct completion records. Application consumption and ownership remain separate from kernel posting. The cancel result tells what happened to the cancellation request; releasing the target buffer still depends on the target’s terminal contract.

## Failure cases

The faulty adapter frees the target buffer when its cancel request completes, then processes a late target completion queue entry. A sanitizer finding identifies a lifetime violation, not kernel exploitability.

Retain the first divergent transition and distinguish the cause from a symptom.

failure is not a successful mutation kill.
