# PR-T71-03: Dual binary session automata with bounded dialogue

## Contract

Implement pt_session_step for both roles, phase and remaining count, rejecting wrong direction/tag and terminal reuse without progress. Build an in-process two-endpoint trace driver using only synthetic messages. The full required specification is {ref}`X-T71-003` and its explicit shared contract. Twelve hours is descriptive author accounting, never a ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

At remaining0, `HELLO` and ACCEPT lead directly to BYE. A client receiving `HELLO` is rejected; an ACK reduces remaining exactly once.

## Steps

- **T71.03:** Complete D1–D4, the named public anchor, independent falsification and changed-contract implementation.

## Failure cases

Enumerate both roles, remaining0..8 and every phase/tag/direction, compare against a separately written transition table and assert rejected-state preservation. Reject direction-erasure and ACK-does-not-decrement mutants.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T71.03:** Satisfy all four leaf rubrics and source-bound verification; passing supplied tests alone is insufficient.

## Hints and recovery

Write a client trace and invert directions for its peer. Represent the next legal action, not the action just completed. An automaton cannot prove that its peer will ever run.

## Transfer challenge

Introduce a choice between retryable rejection and cancellation, plus a peer crash between DATA and ACK. Specify which endpoint detects failure and whether a consumed message can be replayed; compare with the explicitly scoped guarantees of Sesh.
