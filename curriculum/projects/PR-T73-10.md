# PR-T73-10: Demand signaling, terminal events and bounded reentrancy

## Contract

Implement a serialized demand stream with unsigned additive demand, UINT64_MAX unbounded sentinel, next/error/complete signals and cancellation. Emit decrements finite demand before calling next. A next callback may request more or cancel, but recursive emit returns `BUSY`. Request0 signals terminal INVALID. Terminal notification occurs once and no later item is delivered. Integrate with a channel producer using demand credit, leaving overflow/drop policy to T75.

The full required exercise {ref}`X-T73-010`, its shared contracts and objective rubric are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive author accounting, not a learner-time or scope ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Emit with demand0 is `EMPTY`. Request1 then emit99 whose callback requests1 leaves demand1 without recursive delivery; emit7 consumes it. UINT64_MAX then request1 stays unbounded. Complete then error produces one terminal event; later emit is `CLOSED`. Cancellation prevents subsequent emits but does not establish cross-thread callback quiescence.

## Steps

- **T73.10:** Implement explicit demand/terminal/delivering state, saturating unbounded arithmetic and callback ownership; construct a channel adapter that never delivers more items than requested. Submit the exercise D1–D4 and retain prerequisite interfaces.

## Failure cases

Enumerate request/emit/cancel/end traces, test zero and maximum demand, callback requests/cancellation and recursive emission. Reject emission without credit and repeated-terminal mutants. Independently account for requested versus delivered finite items, retain callback context through return and test that end requires no demand.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T73.10:** Derive demand accounting, signal ordering, cancellation and reentrancy limits; compare the original C subset to selected Reactive Streams rules without claiming interface or certification equivalence. Every leaf has a separate rubric criterion in X-T73-010. Public test passage alone does not assess independent understanding.

## Hints and recovery

Consume credit before invoking a reentrant callback. Then Terminal events are not ordinary demand-limited items. Record the failed hypothesis before using further hints.

## Transfer challenge

Make signals originate on several threads: serialize them through one executor and retain the subscription until all in-flight signals quiesce. Delayed assessment introduces a slow subscriber and bounded channel capacity; compare blocking, dropping and failure policies under the same stated delivery contract, with T75 owning the overload decision.
