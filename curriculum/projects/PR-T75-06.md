# PR-T75-06: Health breakers, bulkheads and stale completions

## Contract

Implement RZGate with separate closed/open/half-open health state and active-permit limit1..2. Two consecutive backend failures open for4 ticks; a single half-open probe decides recovery. Each permit carries owner, slot and nonwrapping generation; completion also uses its saved admission epoch so an old success cannot heal a new open period. Add a sliding-window failure-rate policy and a two-tenant bulkhead variant with explicitly reserved capacity, retaining neutral client-error outcomes.

The full required exercise is {ref}`X-T75-006`; its shared contracts, D1–D4, objective-specific rubric and delayed variant are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive accounting, not a ceiling on required work.

## Inputs and expected results

With limit1, a second simultaneous admission returns `BUSY` even while `CLOSED`. Two classified failures open. An older outstanding success releases capacity but leaves the newer `OPEN` state unchanged. At cooldown equality one probe may enter, a second is `BUSY`, and successful probe closes. Repeated completion is `STALE`.

## Steps

- **T75.06:** Implement bounded admission/completion with generation and epoch exhaustion, including late old-epoch results and four-tick cooldown overflow retirement. Complete every exercise deliverable, including separately specified extensions.

## Failure cases

Health decisions summarize observations, while a bulkhead bounds admitted resource ownership. Conflating them allows an otherwise healthy slow backend to exhaust callers or an old completion to erase newer failure evidence. Enumerate admit/fail/succeed/neutral sequences through each finite generation. Test old admission before two failures, then a late success after `OPEN`. Check capacity and exactly-once release after every event. Use an original synthetic overload trace to demonstrate the missing-limit mutant permits excess work; the stale-epoch mutant reopens traffic after a declared trip. Observe counters/status only, not external service load.
