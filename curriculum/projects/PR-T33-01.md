## Contract

Specify and implement a bounded prefix sum for at most 4valuesin[0,10], caller-provided extent and unchanged output on invalid input. Prove its loop invariant and decreasing variant separately from tests.

Use dependency profile C under {ref}`guide-profiles`. Implement the studied mechanism yourself; a mature library may be a separately declared hosted oracle. This 6-hour milestone includes implementation and verification, with reading/assessment budgeted in the module.
