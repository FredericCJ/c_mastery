## Failure cases

Wrong address length, group source, invalid ingress, nonmonotonic time, table full and stale-entry lookup. Local address does not guarantee uniqueness. Returning an ingress port as a flood destination is a forwarding bug. Bridge forwarding is separate from IP routing.
