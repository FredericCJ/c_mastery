# PR-T76-05: Observed-remove sets and replayable delta state

## Contract

Implement RPSet for values0..3 and2 replica writers, each with8 never-reused add identities. adds[value] and removed[value] are16-bit tag sets; next[replica] reserves the next local identity. add returns a delta containing its new tag and counter frontier; remove returns tombstones only for tags actually observed. Merge ORs add/tombstone sets and takes frontier maxima. Empty removal succeeds. Exhausted identity space returns RP_FULL; no reset/reuse while any state/delta survives.

The full required exercise {ref}`X-T76-005`, its shared contracts and objective rubric are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive author accounting, not a learner-time or scope ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Replica0 adds value2; replica1 observes it. Replica0 removes while replica1 concurrently adds its own tag. Join retains value2. Removing after observing both tags and replaying either old add delta does not resurrect it. The ninth add by one replica fails without resetting its identity counter.

## Steps

- **T76.05:** Define membership as adds minus removed. Derive why a concurrent unobserved add survives removal and why an observed add cannot resurrect when its delta arrives again. Treat one writer per replica as a premise. The bounded delta carries exact tag tombstones, so remove-before-add delivery is representable; do not infer this for all causal CRDTs. Explain the delta-interval causal precondition from the paper and specify acknowledgment/stability requirements before reclaiming history. Submit the exercise D1–D4 and retain prerequisite interfaces.

## Failure cases

Run replicated.sets and enumerate two-writer add/remove/delivery traces against a mathematical tagged-set oracle. Include duplicate and remove-before-add delivery. Kill remove-all-possible-tags and omitted-tombstone-merge mutants. Inventory retained tags and show the exact full condition rather than silently dropping history.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T76.05:** Explain add-wins observed removal through unique add identities, retained tombstones and idempotent delta joins. Distinguish a causally self-contained delta representation from arbitrary partial operations or unsafe tombstone collection. Every leaf has a separate rubric criterion in X-T76-005. Public test passage alone does not assess independent understanding.

## Hints and recovery

Give every add a writer and local sequence identity. Then Deliver the removal tombstone before its matching add. Record the failed hypothesis before using further hints.

## Transfer challenge

Introduce a third replica, disconnection and fail-stop termination followed by restart from declared durable state. Design a causal delta-interval anti-entropy protocol with durable incarnation/counter identity and full-state fallback; falsify premature garbage collection with a delayed old delta. Prove a stable frontier before discarding tombstones, or retain them and quantify growth.
