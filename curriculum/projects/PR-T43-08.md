## Inputs and expected results

[The fixture](../../fixtures/performance/PR-T43-08.json) has A=[100,102,103,120,101] and B=[96,99,110,97,98] ns. Medians are 102 and 98; the paired differences [4,3,−7,23,3] have median 3, not their difference of medians 4. Nearest-rank p95 uses ceil(0.95N), giving 120 and 110. A has one strict deadline miss above 110; B has none. Maximum distance from reference 100 is 20 and 10.

Empty-bracket median is 11. Subtracting it from A's median gives sensitivity value 91, not a proved uncontaminated kernel duration. Declared resolution 10 ns is neither accuracy nor a confidence interval.

## Steps

1. Select elapsed and CPU clocks by their contracts: adjustment, suspend inclusion, scope and possible equal readings. Check return statuses, clock_getres and integer seconds/nanoseconds conversion.

2. Extend the existing harness with a controlled paired condition change and at least 31 raw pairs. Preserve alternating/randomized order rationale, warmup and checksum consumption; separate setup.

3. Calculate the named quantile convention, paired differences and reference jitter. Retain outliers with context. Repeat an empty bracket and report sensitivity, including inconclusive values at or below overhead.

4. Write the conclusion with sample count, variation, confounders and scope. A sampled maximum or p95 is an observed statistic, not WCET.

## Failure cases

Reject unequal/empty paired sets and nonfinite/negative durations. A low-resolution clock may return equal timestamps without proving zero work. Integer conversion must not overflow. Process CPU sums across threads and may exceed elapsed time. Do not silently delete an inconvenient slow sample or subtract a negative overhead-corrected result into a speed claim.

## Acceptance and evidence

For .c explain outliers, observed deadline misses/jitter and why the finite sample establishes no universal deadline/WCET guarantee. Keep a link to prior accepted artifacts and a diff explaining the new measurement question. The finite oracle checks the supplied arithmetic only.

## Hints and recovery

Calculate paired differences before sorting either list. Record the quantile rule alongside N; different conventions legitimately differ for a short sample. Increasing batch size may improve signal relative to read overhead but changes the observation boundary. If a result is smaller than overhead variation, report inconclusive evidence and redesign the bracket.
