# PR-T73-04: Actor incarnations, copied mailboxes and failure boundaries

## Contract

Implement a serialized actor with four copied messages, generation-tagged address, run-to-completion handler and explicit death/restart. Enqueue rejects stale incarnation before capacity. A failed handler marks the actor dead and discards queued unsigned messages. Restart is allowed only after handler quiescence and advances generation without wrap. Integrate channel delivery using an explicit single-executor adapter; do not claim the unsynchronized actor struct is safe for concurrent producers.

The full required exercise {ref}`X-T73-004`, its shared contracts and objective rubric are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive author accounting, not a learner-time or scope ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Enqueue2,99,3 for generation1, where handler99 fails. Step2 adds2, step99 fails and queued3 is discarded. Restart creates generation2; generation1 messages are STALE and cannot affect the new actor. Recursive step while a handler is active returns `BUSY`.

## Steps

- **T73.04:** Implement admission, dispatch, death and reincarnation with finite generation retirement; design the channel-to-actor executor boundary and message-drop ownership. Submit the exercise D1–D4 and retain prerequisite interfaces.

## Failure cases

Test full mailbox, stale identity, nested step, failure with queued work, restart while alive and generation exhaustion. Reject stale-generation admission and retained-dead-mailbox mutants. Record effects before failure to show they are not rolled back. Compare actor serialization to a mutex-protected shared object under the same observable ordering.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T73.04:** Explain mailbox ownership, actor identity across restart, run-to-completion serialization and the limit of let-it-crash recovery when effects have already escaped the actor. Every leaf has a separate rubric criterion in X-T73-004. Public test passage alone does not assess independent understanding.

## Hints and recovery

An address can be reused while an incarnation identity must change. Then Removing a message before dispatch avoids replay on a recursive step. Record the failed hypothesis before using further hints.

## Transfer challenge

Messages become owned buffers and handlers may enqueue to themselves: implement deterministic destruction of abandoned messages without recursive dispatch. Delayed assessment receives a late reply addressed to a previous incarnation after restart; route it to an explicit dead-letter sink and prevent delivery to the replacement actor.
