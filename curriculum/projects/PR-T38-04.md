## Steps

1. Write states and the failure/ownership result of every transition. Separate bus addressing from the synthetic register command.

2. Implement bounded scripted replay and output publication. On the F439 single-byte route apply the inspected ACK-off/ADDR-clear/STOP ordering.

3. Add line observation and the conditional bus-clear action; do not generate clocks when SCL is held low.

4. Replay each failure prefix and prove arbitration loss does not force STOP.

5. Repeat the successful bounded transfer on the declared target, observing SDA/SCL; use synthetic fault injection for conditions that cannot safely/reliably be reproduced physically.

## Failure cases

Fail at the first broken phase; terminal-read NACK is not a data_nack. Lost arbitration releases participation. Timeout returns a diagnosed state, not permission to restart another controller’s transaction. An unreleased line after the bounded recovery requests reset handling and reports failure.
