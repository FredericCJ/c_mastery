# PR-T68-07: Derived equality, hashing and shrinking law counterexamples

## Contract

Implement MPKey equality overx,y only; cache is explicitly nonsemantic. Hash canonical little-endian64bit words forx theny with the supplied specified FNV-1a recurrence, not native struct bytes. Implement mp_find_counterexample for pure MPProperty over bounded pairs0..bound, bound<=255: deterministic lexicographic enumeration, first failing input, then decrement-one-coordinate shrinking while failure persists. Return1with locally minimal witness,0if all admitted pairs pass,-1on invalid property/bound; no failure output publication for0/-1.

The full required exercise {ref}`X-T68-007`, its shared contracts and objective rubric are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive author accounting, not a learner-time or scope ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Keys(1,2,cache0) and(1,2,cache9) compareequal and hash equally; (1,3,0) differs. Propertyx+y<5 over0..8 yields a failing pair ofsum5. Reflexivity finds no failure in the bounded domain.

## Steps

- **T68.07:** Prove reflexivity/symmetry/transitivity obligations separately from hashing and collisions. Specify FNV parameters and unsigned64 wrap; this is not a cryptographic integrity claim. The shrinking order finds a local minimum under decrement moves, not necessarily a globally simplest witness for arbitrary nonmonotone predicates. Callback/context must remain live, deterministic and side-effect controlled. Submit the exercise D1–D4 and retain prerequisite interfaces.

## Failure cases

Run metaprog.laws, independently recompute canonical hashes and enumerate bounded law triples. Kill equality-ignores-y and hash-includes-cache mutants. Inject a deliberately wrong equality/hash callback through the learner testing.faults adapter and retain a minimized witness; explain why equal hashes do not imply equal keys.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T68.07:** Explain the equality-implies-equal-hash law, why cached/nonsemantic fields must be excluded consistently, and what deterministic generation plus shrinking establishes and leaves unproved. Every leaf has a separate rubric criterion in X-T68-007. Public test passage alone does not assess independent understanding.

## Hints and recovery
