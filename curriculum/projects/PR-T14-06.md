## Contract

Implement AVL insertion and deletion completely, including single/double rotations and ancestor repair. Stored height equals reconstructed height and every balance magnitude is at most one. Generate small-key permutations and deletion orders against a sorted-set oracle; no library tree delegates are allowed. Keys form a set: duplicate insertion returns a distinct duplicate status and preserves the complete tree and payload ownership without replacement. Storage legality is part of acceptance. Under the selected C17 baseline, an aligned declared unsigned-char array is still a declared character array; alignment alone does not license arbitrary T* lvalue access. The portable byte arena uses byte APIs and explicit copying into/out of actual typed objects. A typed pool uses a real array of the selected T. A hosted harness may separately supply genuine allocated storage through a backing callback, while the studied allocator/container policy stays local. Firmware casts or mapping-backed typed objects need an explicit, inspected implementation contract and are reported under that contract, not as a blanket ISO C guarantee. Check effective type, alignment, bounds and lifetime independently.
The reusable target is Profile C: implement each studied algorithm/container/allocator/byte operation locally. A hosted test oracle may call mature libraries, but the target may not delegate its mechanism to qsort, bsearch, malloc/realloc/free, strto*, printf-style conversion, external memory primitives or equivalent library substitutes. Allocator callbacks/backing storage and compiler helpers must be explicitly scoped and audited.

Worked AVL repair: choose height(empty)=0 and height(leaf)=1, and balance=height(left)-height(right). Inserting 30,20,10 first makes root 30 left-heavy with balance +2. Rotate right at 30: root becomes 20 with leaf children 10 and 30; their heights are 1 and the root height is 2. Duplicate 20 is rejected with the entire structure unchanged. Deleting leaf 30 leaves root 20, left child 10, height 2 and balance +1; ancestor checks still run. More demanding deletion orders can decrease heights through several ancestors, so this anchor does not justify insertion-style early stopping.
