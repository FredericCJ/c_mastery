# PR-T72-08: Readiness-based async frames and lost readiness notifications

## Contract

Implement exact-length asynchronous reads with at most two reads per poll, arm-then-recheck, generation-scoped notifications, synchronous disarm and cancelled/error terminal states. Build an io.reactor/io.stream/io.deadline adapter. The full required specification is {ref}`X-T72-008` and its explicit shared contract. Twelve hours is descriptive author accounting, never a ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

The fake reader returns AGAIN until arm, then supplies all three bytes on the immediate recheck: one poll completes after2 reads,1 arm,1 disarm. A stale generation cannot mark the next frame ready.

## Steps

- **T72.08:** Complete D1–D4, the named public anchor, independent falsification and changed-contract implementation.

## Failure cases

Script readiness arriving between the first AGAIN and arm, repeated spurious wakeups, short progress plus ERROR, EOF, stale notifications and cancel. Preserve final output until complete and count all callbacks; reject omitted recheck and omitted disarm mutants.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T72.08:** Satisfy all four leaf rubrics and source-bound verification; passing supplied tests alone is insufficient.

## Hints and recovery

Write the timeline between observation and registration. A wakeup permits another attempt; it does not reserve bytes. Cancellation cannot reclaim a frame while the reactor still holds its address.

## Transfer challenge

Move notification delivery to another thread and make disarm asynchronous. Introduce a separate quiescence acknowledgment and retained frame ownership, then test all cancellation/completion orders; delayed assessment replaces a borrowed read buffer with a copied channel message.
