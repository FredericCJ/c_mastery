# PR-T65-04: Lazy Result composition and effect-sensitive laws

## Contract

Implement er_map and er_then with exact callback types. A valid error result passes through unchanged without invoking a callback. On OK, map invokes its uint32_t callback once and wraps the value; then invokes its result callback once and preserves that returned result. A missing required callback or unknown tag yields ER_INVALID. Callbacks borrow context and may update a counter but cannot retain it, reenter the operation or destroy the input owner. Derive identity and composition laws under pure, total callback assumptions; test and explain their failure when observable effects differ.

The full required exercise is {ref}`X-T65-004`. Its explicit shared contract, four deliverables, objective rubric, source inspections and delayed variant are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive author accounting, not a study deadline or scope ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Mapping increment over IO(offset7) invokes zero callbacks and preserves7. Mapping over OK(4) produces OK(5) after one invocation; then a rejecting callback produces `RANGE`(offset5). A subsequent then does not call its callback.
