# PR-T74-11: Precise root maps and derived-reference relocation

## Contract

Implement transactional map validation and relocation of every root copy, preserving scalar slots and one-past offsets. Integrate actual runtime.vm roots and temporaries through ADAPTERS.md, then construct and inspect an LLVM statepoint experiment as a separate compiler-dependent artifact. The full required specification is {ref}`X-T74-011` and its explicit shared contract. Twelve hours is descriptive author accounting, never a ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Two root slots with base2 offsets0 and8 and length8 relocate to base5, while scalar2 stays2. A map omitting one tagged root rejects; offset9 rejects the entire frame unchanged. One-past offset8 may be represented but not dereferenced.

## Steps

## Failure cases

Enumerate base/destination/offset combinations, exact maps, missing roots, uninitialized slots, missing forwarding and non-safepoint rejection. Reject omitted-map-validation and omitted-relocation mutants; inspect pre/post values and demonstrate why stale hidden compiler copies require compiler cooperation.

## Hints and recovery

A moving collector must update every usable copy, not merely find one root. Record the allocation base separately from a derived offset. A stack-looking integer is not automatically a pointer.

## Transfer challenge

Add multiple interpreter frames, exceptions and foreign calls that can allocate. Design root registration/unregistration on every exit and safepoint poll protocol. Delayed assessment moves an object while two derived references and a scalar with equal bits are live.
