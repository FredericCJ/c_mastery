## Contract

Implement a target-specific syscall adapter with declared register assignments/clobbers and raw-error conversion. Above it, write-all/read-bounded logic must preserve progress across partial transfers and EINTR, stop on EOF/zero progress and expose would-block policy. Hosted oracles and scripted backends remain outside the custom executable boundary.

## Failure cases

- Challenge the mistaken claim: C-call register assignments can be reused unchanged for syscalls. Preserve a minimal failing trace or explain the valid precondition that excludes it.

- Challenge the mistaken claim: Retrying every error from the beginning is safe. Preserve a minimal failing trace or explain the valid precondition that excludes it.

Exercise empty/singleton or zero-progress states where the operation admits them, exact capacity, one beyond capacity, repeated completion/cleanup and a deliberately invalid precondition. For storage-owning work, inject acquisition failures and verify unchanged old ownership. For build/binary/source studies, inject a wrong edge, symbol, width, target assumption or unsupported environment and require a visible failure verdict. Never execute malformed pointers or privileged instructions just to demonstrate a negative case. Apply these cases to the declared operation; document any inapplicable case and its reason.

## Hints and recovery

Separate the raw Linux negative-error convention from the libc -1/errno convention. Keep the buffer cursor and remaining count across EINTR; do not restart from byte zero after partial progress.

Before changing the target, determine whether the disagreement is its semantics, an invalid test precondition, a boundary mismatch or an environment limitation. If the fixture oracle fails, stop and repair the fixture/oracle independently; do not change expected data to fit the target. Return to the named prerequisite module when ownership, width or ordering assumptions are missing.
