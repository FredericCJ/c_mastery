## Inputs and expected results

Declared Flash-vector reset uses MSP 20002000 hex and vector 08000101 hex, entering 08000100 hex. IRQ 5 is exception 21, vector address 08000054 hex. PSP 20001000 hex gives basic frame base 20000FE0 hex; EXC_RETURN FFFFFFFD selects Thread/PSP basic return. Words restore PC 08000200 hex and LR 08000111 hex; lower number 2 has higher urgency than 7 under the scoped priority convention.
