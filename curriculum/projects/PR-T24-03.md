## Contract

Implement an eight-byte-bounded TCP frame protocol plus a small UDP sequence/loss exercise. Test split/combined frames, half-close, reconnect, datagram truncation, duplicates and reorder. Local send success is not remote acknowledgment; queued bytes and stalled-peer costs have explicit caps.
