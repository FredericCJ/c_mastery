# PR-T74-09: Ephemeron fixed points and staged finalization

## Contract

Implement repeated ephemeron closure, weak clearing before finalizer retention, an explicit pending queue and at-most-once finalization identities. Build a synthetic finalizer driver that may resurrect an object and compare the chosen policy with Lua and CPython. The full required specification is {ref}`X-T74-009` and its explicit shared contract. Twelve hours is descriptive author accounting, never a ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Pairs2→3 then1→2 with strong root1 retain all three only after a fixed point. A pair4→5 whose value5 points back to key4 dies without an external key root. A queued object clears weak references before one finalizer event and may be rooted before the next collection.

## Steps

- **T74.09:** Complete D1–D4, the named public anchor, independent falsification and changed-contract implementation.

## Failure cases

Enumerate small ephemeron relations/root sets independently, include reverse-ordered chains requiring multiple passes, value-key cycles, weak-only roots, pending-queue rejection and resurrection followed by later reclamation. Reject single-pass and missing-finalized-bit mutants.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T74.09:** Satisfy all four leaf rubrics and source-bound verification; passing supplied tests alone is insufficient.

## Hints and recovery

A weak edge does not join the initial strong closure. Revisit ephemerons when new keys become reachable. Finalization policy is observable and differs between runtimes.

## Transfer challenge

Add a finalizer that fails while owning a synthetic resource and one that allocates; specify queue capacity, reentry and eventual cleanup without relying on prompt GC. Delayed assessment changes ephemeron values into graphs containing further ephemeron keys.
