## Failure cases

Zero or negative denominators, out-of-envelope inputs, arithmetic overflow, empty resistor interval and non-binary traces fail explicitly. A low pulse on SDA while SCL high is not ordinary data. A push-pull SCL design is rejected unless the inspected single-controller/no-stretch exception is actually declared.
