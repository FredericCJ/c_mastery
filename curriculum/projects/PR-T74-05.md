# PR-T74-05: Incremental marking and root/store write barriers

## Contract

Implement begin, bounded scan steps, black-source stores and root insertion for the16-object graph. Build an explicit gray-worklist alternative with a capacity proof, and add immediate quiescent sweeping under a stated allocation policy. The full required specification is {ref}`X-T74-005` and its explicit shared contract. Twelve hours is descriptive author accounting, never a ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

With live1..4 and roots1,2, one step blackens1. A subsequent1→3 store grays3, adding root4 grays4, and the remaining three scans finish with all four black. Budget0 performs zero scans.

## Steps

- **T74.05:** Complete D1–D4, the named public anchor, independent falsification and changed-contract implementation.

## Failure cases

Enumerate small graphs/root sets against graph reachability; stop after blackening a root, then insert a white child and add a new white root. Reject missing-object and missing-root barriers, test budget0, cycles and invalid edges, and account for scan work separately from elapsed time.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T74.05:** Satisfy all four leaf rubrics and source-bound verification; passing supplied tests alone is insufficient.

## Hints and recovery

A black object has already had its outgoing edges examined. A new root is an edge from outside the heap. Finishing a snapshot does not authorize later mutation followed by an old sweep.

## Transfer challenge

Permit allocation during an active cycle and compare black allocation, gray allocation and a restart policy. Delayed assessment replaces serialized mutation with a concurrent mutator; derive publication ordering and termination detection rather than adding volatile.
