# PR-T62-03: Shared submission ring arithmetic

## Contract

Implement li_slot(head,tail,capacity,out) for uint32_t monotonic counters: capacity is a power of two in1..2^30, unsigned tail-head must be<capacity, and output is tail&(capacity-1). Reject invalid/full states unchanged. Then map the arithmetic to raw io_uring offsets and acquire/release operations after feature probing.

Shared requirements: [B06 exercise contract](../v5/contracts/b06.md). Stable
unit IDs are `LAB-LI-03A` and `LAB-LI-03B`. Families: A, B, C, D, F, G, I, J;
source records: R-B06-URING in
`research/v5/b06_resources.json`, using only its inspected locators.

## Failure cases

A producer masks tail and overwrites a still-owned full slot. Adding a barrier cannot repair missing capacity admission.

Retain the first divergent transition and distinguish the cause from a symptom.
