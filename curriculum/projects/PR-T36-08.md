## Failure cases

Include inconsistent comparator, context change while populated, borrowed-record lifetime expiry, mutation of its rank while stored and callback reentry. These are rejected client contracts/diagnostics; never execute dangling-pointer UB. Define failure-atomicity at the adapter boundary, including backing callback failure. Typed copies remain independent of later caller-value changes, whereas borrowed mutation must first remove the object.
