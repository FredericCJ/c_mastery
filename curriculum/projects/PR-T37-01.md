## Inputs and expected results

The fixture covers 7+1, 15+1, 8−1, 0−1 and all eight full-adder rows. Exhaust all 256 operand pairs for each operation: addition has 120 carry and 64 overflow pairs; subtraction has 120 borrow and 64 overflow pairs. Four-bit results are not actual x86 register widths; the relationship is in flag semantics. Declare model outputs separately from any 32-bit ISA checks.

## Steps

Derive sum/carry equations from the truth table, compose slices and track carry into/out of the sign bit. Compute signed overflow once from sign relations and once from independently widened range arithmetic. Exhaust every pair and minimize each discrepancy. Create a paired x86/Cortex-M architectural-state table and classify every submitted observation as model, executable ISA or physical measurement. Run one defined unsigned 32-bit target compare as optional corroboration, never an overflowing signed-C test.

## Failure cases

Reject operands outside 0..15 or unsupported operations without changing prior output. Include carry-without-overflow, overflow-without-carry, subtraction borrow polarity, negative zero misconception and a deliberately swapped carry/V wire. Hardware wrap cannot be used to excuse undefined C signed overflow.

## Hints and recovery

Use 7+1 and 15+1 to separate signed overflow from carry. On subtraction, invert the borrow sense when presenting Arm C. Range-check signed interpretations in a wider type; do not use the target’s overflowing signed expression as your oracle.
