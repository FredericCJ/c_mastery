## Contract

Specify operands, tied/early-clobber needs and clobbers for one small inline assembly experiment, retaining safe defined C inputs. Compare compiler barrier and processor ordering responsibilities. Classify normal calls, userspace syscalls and privileged exception paths; do not execute privileged instructions on the study host.

## Failure cases

- Challenge the mistaken claim: volatile asm or a memory clobber automatically acts as a hardware fence. Preserve a minimal failing trace or explain the valid precondition that excludes it.
- Challenge the mistaken claim: Every instruction visible in disassembly is legal in userspace. Preserve a minimal failing trace or explain the valid precondition that excludes it.

Exercise empty/singleton or zero-progress states where the operation admits them, exact capacity, one beyond capacity, repeated completion/cleanup and a deliberately invalid precondition. For storage-owning work, inject acquisition failures and verify unchanged old ownership. For build/binary/source studies, inject a wrong edge, symbol, width, target assumption or unsupported environment and require a visible failure verdict. Never execute malformed pointers or privileged instructions just to demonstrate a negative case. Apply these cases to the declared operation; document any inapplicable case and its reason.
