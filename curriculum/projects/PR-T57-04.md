# PR-T57-04: Context-sensitive locking

## Contract

Implement kl_lock_allowed(context,kind,rt) for conservative teaching policy: context0 task,1 hardirq,2 softirq; kind0 mutex,1 spinlock_t,2 raw_spinlock_t. Mutex is task-only; raw lock is allowed in all three; ordinary spin lock is allowed in all contexts without `PREEMPT_RT` and only task context in this restricted `PREEMPT_RT` exercise. Reject out-of-range fields. Defend why the policy is deliberately narrower than all Linux APIs.
