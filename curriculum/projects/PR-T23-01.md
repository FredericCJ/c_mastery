## Contract

Implement a fork/exec/wait launcher with a child-error pipe, deliberate close-on-exec and exact descriptor ownership. Capture process context and an interface-scope manifest. A failed exec reports to the parent and terminates the child path; threaded post-fork restrictions must be analyzed before reuse in a threaded process.
