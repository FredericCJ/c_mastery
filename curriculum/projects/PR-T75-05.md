# PR-T75-05: Jittered retry schedules with a shared deadline and attempt budget

## Contract

Implement RZRetry schedule with bounded attempts, capped exponential windows and injected full-jitter draws. Reject unsafe-to-repeat operations and starts at or beyond the inherited deadline. Implement a deterministic PRNG adapter with unbiased bounded selection using rejection sampling; keep its seed record and entropy limitations explicit. Connect scheduled attempts to the accepted communications.tcp_fault loss/duplicate/late-ack model, charging every dispatch to a common retry limiter at one chosen layer.

The full required exercise is {ref}`X-T75-005`; its shared contracts, D1–D4, objective-specific rubric and delayed variant are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive accounting, not a ceiling on required work.

## Inputs and expected results

Base2/cap8 produces draw windows[0,2],[0,4],[0,8]. At now9 with deadline10, draw1 rejects and preserves attempt count. A non-idempotent request rejects before scheduling. Three admitted retries exhaust a maximum3 budget even if all draws were zero.

## Steps

## Failure cases

Backoff can amplify overload while making average latency look better. Randomizing when an unsafe operation repeats does not make its effects idempotent. Use arbitrary-precision min(cap,base*2^k) and a finite draw enumeration oracle. For unbiased selection, enumerate a small synthetic random source and count accepted residues exactly; do not equate one histogram with proof of randomness. Trace original attempt plus retries, limiter charges, pending completions and cancellation. Security demonstration remains synthetic retry amplification in the teaching model, with bounded requests and no external endpoint.
