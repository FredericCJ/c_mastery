# PR-T64-01: Fixed-point scale, rounding and saturation

## Contract

Implement ns_q15(a,b) for signed Q1.15 operands: multiply exactly in int32_t, round the scaled result to nearest with ties away from zero, then saturate into [-32768,32767]. In particular -32768 times -32768 saturates to32767. Derive the range of every intermediate and distinguish quantization error from saturation.

## Steps

- **T64.01:** Fixed-point multiplication changes scale twice: the product has twice as many fractional bits, and rounding precedes a checked narrowing. Saturating after a signed overflow is not a repair.
- **T64.01:** Implement ns_q15(a,b) for signed Q1.15 operands: multiply exactly in int32_t, round the scaled result to nearest with ties away from zero, then saturate into [-32768,32767]. In particular -32768 times -32768 saturates to32767. Derive the range of every intermediate and distinguish quantization error from saturation.
- **T64.01:** Derive the product scale and prove int32_t multiplication and negation bounds before implementing absolute value. Separate saturation policy from the rounding rule.

## Failure cases

The mutant truncates toward zero, failing positive and negative half-way cases. A signed right shift is not a portable replacement for the specified rounding rule.
