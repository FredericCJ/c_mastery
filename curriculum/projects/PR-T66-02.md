# PR-T66-02: Scoped cleanup, conditional undo and publication by swap

## Contract

Implement an eight-entry OwScope with ow_defer(fn,context,when) and ow_finish(success), where when is `ALWAYS`, `ON_ERROR` or `ON_SUCCESS` and success is exactly0 or1. Reject a full stack or registration during finish without changing it. Pop each entry before calling its callback, execute applicable callbacks in reverse registration order, and empty the stack even when a conditional callback is skipped. Finish on empty is idempotent. Implement ow_publish for two disjoint valid owners: the staged owner must be fully constructed, publication transfers it to the destination and then releases the former destination. No callback failure, reentrancy or longjmp is admitted.

The full required exercise {ref}`X-T66-002`, its shared contracts and objective rubric are part of this project. Eight hours is descriptive author accounting, not a learner-time or scope ceiling.

## Inputs and expected results

Register digits1 `ALWAYS`,2 `ON_SUCCESS`,3 `ON_ERROR`. Failure produces trace31; success produces21; a second finish appends nothing. Publishing three staged blocks over three old blocks leaves exactly three live blocks and consumes the staged owner.

## Steps

- **T66.02:** Implement conditional LIFO cleanup and publication-by-swap with explicit callback effects, stack capacity, transferred ownership and nonlocal-exit restrictions. Submit the exercise D1–D4 and retain prerequisite interfaces.

## Failure cases

Enumerate all success/failure policy combinations through depth4 against an independent reversed-list filter, test capacity8/9 and callback reentrancy rejection, inject failure before every registration, and trace publication before old cleanup. Kill a mutant that executes every conditional cleanup.

## Acceptance and evidence

- **T66.02:** Explain when a cleanup stack is a resource protocol, when an undo log is a semantic rollback protocol, and why neither automatically reverses already published external effects. Every leaf has a separate rubric criterion in X-T66-002. Public test passage alone does not assess independent understanding.

## Hints and recovery

Success and failure policies select entries; they do not alter stack order. Then Remove an entry before calling user code. Record the failed hypothesis before using further hints.

## Transfer challenge

Integrate cleanup.scope with the T65 typed jump boundary through an adapter that converts a raised error before final cleanup. Delayed assessment introduces a cleanup action that can fail: derive an explicit recovery-required result and retained owner rather than silently pretending the old void-callback contract still applies.
