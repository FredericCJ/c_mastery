## Resolved design issues

Ownership/API foundations now precede container design. Firmware concurrency uses selected memory-model preludes rather than an unnecessary full pthread prerequisite. T01 is diagnostic with conditional remediation, and mathematics is repaired just before the relevant numerical/timing work. Large integration projects are split into small milestones; shared implementations are charged once.

Historical source assumptions, incorrect edition pagination, draft labels, board/manual revisions and the F439 RCC chapter are explicit. Library-free claims require an actual dependency boundary. The website and standalone Markdown derive from the same canonical teaching sources; publication checks inspect objective anchors, links, assets and retained prose.

## Limits of this deliverable

The deliverable is a curriculum with fixtures and assessment contracts. It does not claim that a learner has passed them, that target C implementations have been supplied, or that firmware hardware tests have already succeeded. Publishing/fixture/compiler checks are separate from learner implementation gates.

Hardware timing, electrical behavior, boot/update failure experiments and actual peripheral acceptance require the recorded real setup. The MPS2 emulator cannot substitute for STM32 behavior. Formal results require learner-executed tools, pinned models/solvers and explicit obligations. Measured maxima are not automatically WCET bounds.

Some source passages are historical or pre-publication. Inaccessible bibliographic leads remain visible and unassignable. New chapters and deeper elective implementations require additional inspection. Mixed-module overhead is conservatively counted in the core; personal schedules may reduce remedial/elective work without erasing awareness objectives.

## Before claiming completion

Resolve every core objective's evidence checklist, defend both capstones, repeat delayed transfer checks and record all pending physical gates. Inspect unresolved questions rather than replacing them with optimistic labels. A core portfolio with pending board evidence remains incomplete for the firmware branch.
