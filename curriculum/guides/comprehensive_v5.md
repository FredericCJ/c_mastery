# Comprehensive fifth-edition assessment extension

This additive guide governs the active comprehensive expansion alongside the
retained [assessment guide](assessment.md). Historical acceptance records and
their source hashes remain unchanged.

Historical awareness and elective labels describe the earlier gate; they do
not exclude those topics from required comprehensive scope. Complete each
applicable supplemental construction, diagnosis, boundary, verification and
transfer criterion in the expansion catalog. Preserve the earlier receipt and
record the additional evidence separately. Historical hours are descriptive
accounting, never a study-time ceiling.

An authored rubric or passing reference implementation does not establish
independent learner performance. Assess explanation, derivation, implementation,
diagnosis, verification, measurement and transfer under changed assumptions.
A finite collection of passing tests does not prove absolute mastery.

Use the [implementation inventory](../v5/expansion/work_inventory.json) for
originating obligations and six distinct evidence dimensions. Read its exact
requirements and linked contracts: a source row or a coverage heading alone
does not establish completion. Delayed variants with local labels such as V1
retain their original exercise scope; similarly named variants in different
exercises are different obligations.

Author implementation, author execution, external review, independent learner
attempts and physical target observations remain separate. Missing external
evidence does not excuse completing the corresponding executable assessment
package, nor unrelated author work. Reference imports must be identified as
reference imports; subsequent integration with a learner's actual artifacts
requires its own source identities and evidence.
