# Select mechanisms under an unfamiliar workload

The three recurring systems are the Linux telemetry service, firmware acquisition
gateway and VM/store appliance. Each consumes earlier learner artifacts through
versioned contracts. A supplied reference adapter permits author validation; it
does not establish that a learner built or qualified the imported component.

At an integration boundary, provide the workload, resource ceilings, failure
contract and observation scope without naming the required structure. Ask for an
abstract contract first, then two plausible representations, a correctness and
cost argument, a C ownership plan and a discriminating experiment. Reject choices
that satisfy average throughput by violating retention or cancellation bounds.

Keep a visible theory strand. Loop invariants and induction support traversal and
mutation; recurrences support divide-and-conquer; amortized proofs account for
resizing and deque work; exchange arguments support greedy choices; state
sufficiency and recurrence design support dynamic programming; probabilistic
assumptions support sketches; comparison lower bounds clarify which key model an
optimization changes. Require the argument explicitly: implementations alone do
not assess those claims.

Changed assumptions must add depth: finite capacity, allocation failure,
concurrency, external-memory cost, precision, ownership or recovery. Cosmetic
parameter changes are not separate encounters. Preserve small exhaustive models,
then bridge to configurable bounds and the integrated consumer's actual lifecycle.

For independent learner validation, use separate packets for streaming, deadlines
and representation/performance. Withhold the final transfer workload and keep
assessor solutions outside learner archives. Observe unstated prerequisites,
misread contracts, dependence on copied implementations and independent diagnosis.
Record assistance, first attempts and delayed attempts accurately. Authoring a
packet is executable work; independent participants and educational effectiveness
remain uncollected evidence until real observation occurs.
