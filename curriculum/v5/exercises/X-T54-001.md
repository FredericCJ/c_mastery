## Security

Target: the peer submit/ack/restart/ready code in `labs/b045_systems` and the stale-ACK variant you build, as a local process over synthetic peer metadata. No device is reset and nothing is flashed.

Adversary: you supply ACK(epoch,seq) pairs, notably a stale ACK(1,1) after Submit(1,1) and a restart, and you may restart with or without trusted quiescence. You do not control the epoch counter, the ready flag or the retained result record.

Violated property: an acknowledgement from an old epoch must not complete a request from a new epoch, and restart without quiescence must not erase the uncertainty about the in-flight request.

Evidence to capture: run `depth-tests peer` (CTest `b045_systems.peer`) on the correct build and record the status and struct s_peer fields at each CHECK: restart without quiescence returns S_PENDING with `p.outstanding`; trusted restart gives `p.epoch == 2`, `p.uncertain`, no outstanding work and `!p.ready`; `s_peer_ready(&p, 1)` is S_CONFLICT; ACK(1,1) is S_CONFLICT with work still outstanding; ACK(2,1) succeeds once and a repeat is S_CONFLICT; restart at epoch UINT32_MAX is S_CONFLICT. Then build a variant whose ACK compares only the sequence and record that the test exits 1 with `DEPTH_CHECK:<line>:s_peer_ack(&p, 1, 1) == S_CONFLICT && p.outstanding`.

Impact class: demonstrated-invariant-violation, shown only by the seq-only-ACK variant you build; the supplied test passes on a correct build and no supplied artifact demonstrates the violation. It is a falsely completed local record, not a crash or sanitizer finding, and not proved-impact since no device or real data is affected.

Remediation: check both identifiers, require trusted quiescence before advancing the epoch, and keep the uncertainty record. The regression test is the existing `b045_systems.peer`; it must fail on your seq-only-ACK variant and on a restart-ignores-trusted-flag variant, and pass on the repair.

Limitations: Does not model a real peer, timing, lost resets or a peer that lies about quiescence; the supervisor/request-ownership composition is argued in D1, not executed; only the fields of struct s_peer are modelled.
