## Problem

Implement b3_due with8-bit ticks and delay0..127. start250,now4,delay10 is due; now128,start0 is AMBIGUOUS and preserves output. Exhaust all256 starts,128 true elapsed intervals and128 delays. The caller separately promises true elapsed<128; provide two histories with identical counter samples but different elapsed time to prove a missed full wrap cannot be detected by this function.

Use supplied include/depth.h, src/depth.c, tests/tests.c and fixtures/b03_depth/cases.json. The complete API, pointer, state, ownership and failure contract in labs/b03_depth/CONTRACTS.md is explicitly incorporated. Preserve all admission and termination rules.

## Necessity

Comparing absolute wrapped numbers confuses order. Modular subtraction solves a bounded elapsed problem, not recovery of arbitrary chronology. Casting an out-of-range unsigned difference to signed is not a portable proof.

## Reasoning

Derive modulo256 subtraction with integer promotions/conversion, then use unwrapped elapsed e in[0,127] as the independent model. State units/resolution and distinguish equality, future, due and ambiguous. Explain why full-wrap aliases evade validation and require an external sampling or wider-counter guarantee. Separate measured maxima from WCET.

Distinguish enforcement by C, diagnostics, runtime state checks, proof/model and caller/environment discipline. Show one plausible wrong explanation and a minimal counterexample. State precisely what each experiment cannot establish.
