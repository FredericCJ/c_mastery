## Reasoning

Released-high but observed-low while SCL is high indicates another controller won; a ninth-bit low is instead receiver ACK because role/phase changed. Low SCL does not complete a bit. Losing a byte does not authorize injecting STOP into the winning transaction. The primitive deliberately has no analog timing or MCU register claims.
