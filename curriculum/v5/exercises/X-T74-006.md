## Security

A copying collector moves objects, so every reference must be rewritten to the new location, with sharing and cycles preserved, or the program keeps pointing into the abandoned space. The attacker chooses the roots, the young and old graph (shared targets, cycles, old-to-young edges), the capacities given to each collection and the order of collections. The target is your own `or_minor` and `struct or_nursery` in `labs/object_runtime`, a modelled heap of two 8-cell young semispaces and an 8-cell old array; the forwarding table and collector code are not under attacker control.

Why it matters here. The contract (`labs/object_runtime/CONTRACTS.md`, nursery section) evacuates roots, then fields of old objects that point to young cells, then the fields of every newly copied cell, and publishes the new space only after all of them are rewritten. If old-to-young edges are skipped, an old object keeps an index into the discarded semispace. If the capacity is too small, the whole original heap and the copied count must be left exactly as they were.

What to try. For the anchor heap (root 3 reaching a young cell that leads to a second young cell whose edge points at itself, and an old object pointing at that second cell), predict the roots, edges and payloads after the first minor collection, after an attempt with old capacity 1, and after the second collection that promotes survivors. Run `ctest -R object_runtime.nursery`: its `nursery` function pins two cells copied, `roots[0] == 1`, `old[0].edge[0] == 2`, payloads 30 and 20 with the shared edge kept, `OR_FULL` with the copied count still 91 and the heap byte-identical, and after promotion `old_used == 3`, `roots[0] == 10` and both old edges 11. Then build two variants of your own, one that never rewrites old-to-young edges and one that leaves a root pointing at its old location, and note which CHECKs fail. The preflight and rollback designs required by the other tests are separate and must keep these CHECKs passing.

What to capture and how to read it. Keep the `OR_CHECK:<line>:<expression>` text, the references before and after each collection and the byte snapshot around `OR_FULL`. A reference naming an index in the abandoned space, or two references to one object becoming two copies, is the demonstration. Record commands and source hashes. The assessor's mutation set also checks this invariant.

Impact class. `demonstrated-invariant-violation`, only against a variant you build: a stale or unrewritten reference in the modelled heap. No relocated cell is reused or exploited, and nothing crashes or hits undefined behaviour.

Remediation and regression. Rewrite roots, old-to-young fields and copied fields through the forwarding table before publishing the new space and keep capacity failure atomic. Keep the graphs that exposed your variants as regressions.

Limitations. Raw pointers across a collection, concurrent mutators and a real handle adapter are not shown, and the synthetic payload is not a persistent object identity.
