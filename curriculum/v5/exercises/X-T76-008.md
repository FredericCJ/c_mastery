# X-T76-008: Field tags, minimal varints and canonical bytes

## Prerequisites

Complete the earlier domain projects and retain the named prerequisite artifacts with their contracts, ownership ledger and tests. Required learner artifacts: codec.records, io.frame, testing.faults. Do not import an assessor implementation.

## Environment

Implement mechanisms locally using declared typed storage/callbacks; hosted harness and diagnostic runtimes are separate. See laboratory contract for exact dependencies. Use C17 with extensions disabled and warnings treated as errors. A diagnostic extension is identified explicitly and does not become an ISO guarantee. The full platform, pointer, callback and concurrency preconditions are in [replicated/CONTRACTS.md](../../../labs/replicated/CONTRACTS.md).

## Problem

Implement RPMessage with up to8 unique fields, tags1..15 and uint32 values. Wire keys are(field<<3)|0 and all values use minimal base128 uint32 varints. Fields1 and2 are required; field1 is an ID and field2 is a ZigZag-encoded signed delta; unknown advisory3..15 fields are retained semantically. Reject duplicates, other wire types, critical tags>=16, overlong/nonminimal/truncated/overflow varints, missing required fields and excessive field count. Decode accepts any field order; encode sorts tags, preserves unknown values and publishes bytes/length only on success. This is a stated teaching subset, not general Protocol Buffers compatibility.

Concrete inputs and expected results: Bytes18 07 10 01 08 96 01 (hex) decode and canonicalize to08 96 01 10 01 18 07. 08 80 00 10 01 is rejected as nonminimal. ZigZag(-1)=1, INT32_MIN maps to UINT32_MAX, INT32_MAX maps to UINT32_MAX-1.

Supplied artifacts: labs/replicated/include/replicated.h, compiling unsolved src/replicated.c, CTest replicated.wire, fixtures/replicated/PR-T76-08.json, and the shared contracts. State every additional backend assumption before running it.

## Necessity

Distinguish a wire grammar from one canonical encoding and show why deterministic serialization under one binary is not a universal content identity. Derive portable signed ZigZag arithmetic without signed-left-shift assumptions. The misconception and changed-condition case below are separately assessed; ordinary successful execution alone does not cover them.

## Reasoning

For five-byte uint32 varints require the final payload<=15 and no continuation. Derive ZigZag using unsigned conversion/shift and a sign mask; decode odd values as -(value>>1)-1 to include INT32_MIN safely. Prove canonical decode/reencode idempotence and uniqueness for the admitted semantic map. Explain the tradeoff of rejecting duplicate fields compared with protocols that merge repeated occurrences. Give the abstraction function, admitted states, pre/postconditions, ownership graph, failure effects and complexity before implementation. Identify the enforcement boundary of every safety claim using the shared modern-cost contract. Distinguish an invalid caller object from an operational failure admitted by the API.

## Deliverables

- D1: Distinguish a wire grammar from one canonical encoding and show why deterministic serialization under one binary is not a universal content identity. Derive portable signed ZigZag arithmetic without signed-left-shift assumptions.
- D2: For five-byte uint32 varints require the final payload<=15 and no continuation. Complete all thirteen cost/enforcement dimensions with evidence or a mechanism-specific non-applicability.
