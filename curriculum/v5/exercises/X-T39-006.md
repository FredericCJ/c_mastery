## Problem

Implement ed_rx_acquire/release. Two snapshots with capacities62/64, firstFS, lastLS and FL100 expose62+34 bytes after one total FCS subtraction. FL64 with firstcapacity62 exposes60+0: all meaningful data precedes a straddling FCS. Withhold finalLS, then submit LS-only late data; neither publishes a borrow. Inject OWN/error/stale/duplicate release and UINT32_MAX generation.
