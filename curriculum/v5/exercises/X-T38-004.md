## Problem

Implement the SDI2c phase machine and bus-clear model. Address0x50 begins with A0, register07 follows its ACK, RESTART_READ selects A1, the read-byte action generates terminal NACK+STOP and publishes5A only on BYTE completion. Inject every error at each admitted phase. Arbitration loss emits RELEASE without STOP. SCL low requires RESET and zero pulses; SDA low with release_after3 uses three pulses; continued low uses nine then RESET. Register and final-data output uses are specified by action, not an untyped success assumption.

## Necessity

The first byte NACK, register NACK and final receiver NACK have different meanings. Issuing STOP after losing arbitration disrupts another controller. Nine clocks are a conditional recovery attempt, not a universal cure. Token reuse admits a stale completion unless bounded exhaustion is explicit.
