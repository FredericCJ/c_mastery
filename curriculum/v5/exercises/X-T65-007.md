## Problem

Implement er_add16 over uint32_t inputs constrained to0–65535. Checked overflow returns ER_RANGE with output unchanged; wrapping returns the mathematical sum modulo65536; saturating returns min(sum,65535). Reject other inputs or policies. Implement er_serial16(a,b,order): equal gives0, forward distance in1–32767 gives1, distance32769–65535 gives-1; exact half-range32768 is ambiguous and returns ER_RANGE without changing order. This relation is not a total transitive ordering over unrestricted populations. Use no signed overflow or out-of-range signed conversion.

Concrete expected cases: 65535+1 gives rejection/0/65535 under checked/wrapping/saturating policies. Serial0 is forward of65535; 32768 versus0 is ambiguous. Derive a three-value cycle to refute use as an unrestricted sorting comparator.

Supplied artifacts: labs/errors/include/errors.h, compiling unsolved src/errors.c, the named CTest errors.arithmetic, and fixtures/errors/PR-T65-07.json. No installed package may supply the mechanism under assessment.

## Necessity

Derive the difference between mathematical range, unsigned modular semantics, saturation and the bounded-window assumption of serial comparisons. An ordinary nominal call does not assess this distinction. The two isolated mutants and the changed-condition case expose different incorrect explanations.
