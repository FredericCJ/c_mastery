## Problem

Implement sd_uart, sd_uart_decode and sd_uart_receive. For 42MHz/115200 produce N365, BRR0x16D or OVER8 0x2D5; 8N1 A5 gives 0101001011. Include asymmetric 01 to expose reversed bit order, 7/8 payload width, parity and stop errors. A 3Mbaud request is invalid under OVER16 but valid under OVER8. Return complete failure canaries unchanged. Distinguish width from the hardware M bit, and derive actual baud/ppm without rounding away the discrepancy.
