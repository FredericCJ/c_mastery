## Necessity

UaTx/R5Channel admit at most16bytes. Six calls would release DE and add six
turnarounds, changing the frame/grant contract. TXE merely permits another write;
it cannot acknowledge the last stop bit. Starting a retry/end deadline at an
offer or treating the2ms first-byte deadline as the whole reply deadline changes
which exchanges are legal. Ignoring echo does not establish that its UART status
was valid or that another physical driver was silent.

## Representation, ownership and reasoning

EpTx owns96 copied bytes, one actual PuDriver and fixed counters. One serialized
foreground owner controls its UART and DE; callbacks terminate, do not reenter,
retain pointers, allocate or dispatch another owner. Inputs/output/control
objects are live, correctly aligned and disjoint. EpMaster owns that EpTx,
one E7Grant and one actual EfFramer. Exposed state is inspectable but not forgeable
by contract. ISO C cannot enforce callback discipline or actual clock accuracy.

Open requires zeroed, fresh state and exclusive quiescent resources. Selected
ports require a correct stable PuClock snapshot; open may configure registers
before failure, so failure is not a transactional hardware rollback. Reopening
live state, erasing retirement history and concurrent ISR mutation are invalid.
After successful send, caller bytes may be released; BUSY/STALE/INVALID/FULL or
the fit refusal preserve the existing transfer. Tokens increase without wrap,
including the twelve possible internal submissions. Exhaustion retires the
owner after quiescence. There is no DMA path in this adapter.

Time is nonwrapping uint32 microseconds, nondecreasing per owner, at most
UINT32_MAX-30000 (poll admission additionally leaves60000us). The grant producer
has the same nonwrapping limitation. Polling gaps have no hidden hardware-time
guarantee. Fit uses actual OVER16 BRR, not requested baud. Each call is bounded;
send O(n<=96), step O(1), receive one bounded frame operation, fixed memory.
Retained frame-sum stack analysis excludes recursion and concurrent interrupts.

## Failure, timing and publication

IDLE→ENABLE→ACTIVE→GUARD→IDLE. Enable waits max(200us EDU7 policy, rounded selected maximum); the
UART budget starts at that enable due time. At or after the local TX budget the
adapter initiates cancellation before another write, then waits indefinitely
for actual lower memory/line release. It retains DE/state during unknown TC;
deadline is not permission to free or physically force the bus idle. A caller
cancel during enable writes no byte. Cancel during final guard cannot retract a
completed transmission. Intermediate TC permits the next eight-byte submission
without a DE edge. Final TC permits DE low; max(rounded disable,200us guard)
precedes terminal return. Terminal status is reported once; later step is STALE.

`ep_poll` queues an actual master1→slave2 POLL. At the actual final-TC/DE-low
transition, `ep_tick` begins E7Grant. The producer's200us peer-start guard and
the local200us post-DE guard cover the same interval. Neither a second guard
nor the transceiver's shorter enable maximum substitutes for original EDU7 policy. The first-byte window
ends at origin+2200; a started reply has an inclusive10000us completion window
from its first received byte. No next turn begins before origin+25000.

`ep_receive` consumes supplied RXNE observations. Faults are counted before echo
classification and invalidate the partial parser; a faulted echo increments
both counters without peer publication. Fault-free echo during any local TX
phase is ignored. Clean peer bytes require the current grant; after complete
CRC/canonical-frame validation, actual link admission and source2/destination1
checks, DATA publishes one copied frame. EMPTY completes the grant without data.
Foreign/malformed data does not become publication. Saturated counters refuse
before consuming the observation. `ep_disconnect` is an explicit software loss
notice, not proof of a cable sensor; it invalidates partial state and starts
local cancellation if necessary. Reconnect needs a fresh legal grant.

The complementary EpSlave admits a clean actual POLL from master1 to slave2;
it owns a separate parser and grant origin at the received final delimiter.
`ep_reply` requires that permission, waits200us, requires enough enable time to
start by origin+2200 and consumes the permission exactly once on copied lower
admission. A stalled first-byte step after that boundary initiates cancellation
before writing any byte. DATA or EMPTY uses the actual encoder and the same96
byte owner. A duplicate POLL during an unexpired permission cannot extend its
window. These local control frames are not authenticated freshness capabilities.
`ep_master_poll_rx` and `ep_slave_poll_rx` additionally acquire one observation
through the actual PuRx `pu_rx_poll`/`pu_take` producer, preserving status with
the byte and the destructive status-then-data register-read order. Master counter
headroom is reserved before that acquisition. A full counter cannot be repaired
by pretending hardware will preserve unread input indefinitely.

## Selected ports and physical proof

Port0 imports actual USART3 PD8/PD9 AF7, PD10 DE. Port1 adds USART2 PA2/PA3 AF7,
PA1 DE on F439; both keep /RE low, use push-pull low-speed output and preserve
unrelated GPIO fields. RCC GPIO/APB enable readbacks precede access. PA1/PA2
conflict with Ethernet/reference-clock or MDIO usage: reserve these pins and
disable conflicting board connections before a separately authorized bench.
The bridge owns both ports; endpoint boards may use port0. Two segments are
electrically separate A/B pairs with shared declared references and two end
terminations each. Concurrent DE on different segments is legal; it proves
nothing about overlapping drivers on one segment.

The changed THVD1410 part has a600ns selected enable maximum with /RE low versus
650ns for THVD1400; both round to1us here, below the separate200us pre-DE policy. Receiver-disabled enable limits differ
and are not this adapter profile. Do not borrow one part's bus voltage envelope
or packaging argument. Record analog uncertainty and actual supply/load, not
only timing numbers. Follow BOARD_CAPTURE.md; no board is flashed by this work.
