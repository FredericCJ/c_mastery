## Representation, admission and lifetime

`struct xi_record` contains sequence, timestamp, original length, copied length, flags and eight inline bytes. It owns copied bytes; it does not retain the source pointer. `struct xi_queue` owns up to eight records and two atomic unsigned indices. Admit N=2,4,8, reject zero, singleton, non-powers and larger capacities. Usable capacity is N−1. Head and tail always lie in `[0,N)`; equal means empty and next(head)==tail means full. One nonreentrant producer owns head and producer phase; one nonreentrant consumer owns tail and consumer phase. Initialized queue storage, records and indices must remain alive and naturally aligned through all accesses. Initialization/destruction requires both roles quiescent. API objects and input/output records must not overlap queue storage; pointer validity is a caller precondition, not established by integer checks.

The producer progresses idle → reserved → filled → published/idle. `reserve` reads availability, `fill` copies at most one fixed record, and `commit` publishes head. A premature commit returns STATE. Cancellation abandons an unpublished slot. The consumer progresses idle → copied-but-held → released/idle. `take` copies the record into independent caller storage but retains the slot until `release`; a second take returns BUSY. Invalid/full/empty/state refusals preserve output records and published indices. Producer and consumer may pause arbitrarily between these phases without borrowing the other's active slot. They may not recursively enter their own role. The supported compiled ABI has 32-bit unsigned and always-lock-free atomic unsigned; these are admitted assumptions, not universal ISO guarantees.

`struct xi_service` owns the producer role exclusively. `capture_begin` admits a nonrecursive event, records one logical acknowledgement, reserves storage and copies `min(length,8)` stable input bytes. Length may be UINT32_MAX provided those first eight bytes exist. Zero length may use a null pointer. `original_length` retains the full length and TRUNCATED identifies loss; no deferred access to original storage occurs. `capture_end` publishes. Full queue means acknowledged and dropped, with no deferred record. A nested call returns BUSY, increments the reentrant counter, and does not acknowledge the already owned event. Counters saturate at UINT32_MAX; saturation preserves a lower bound, not an exact count beyond that point.

The software active flag is **not** an atomic test-and-set. The target serializes the complete `capture_begin` with saved PRIMASK. The controlled higher-priority entrant occurs after successful begin and before end, when it can safely observe BUSY. Arbitrary instruction-level recursion without that entry serialization is outside the contract. The target driver separately performs the actual peripheral W1C acknowledgement; incrementing a software counter is not a hardware acknowledgement. No other producer may use the same queue behind the service.

## T29.04 — Which actors are excluded?

One naturally atomic word, or one C atomic per field, does not make the pair one atomic transaction. Compare with a cooperating hosted mutex around the entire pair.
