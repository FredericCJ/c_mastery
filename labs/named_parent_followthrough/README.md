# Remaining systems, target and scheduling evidence

The original lifecycle, bring-up, peripheral, systems-target and scheduling
tasks remain required. Their ordinary and delayed contracts retain their
original IDs and assessment criteria. The exact mapping is recorded in
`research/v5/expansion/named_parent_followthrough/evidence_map.json`.

For every experiment, distinguish a source reading, a mathematical model, a
compiler/object inspection, an actual CPU execution and an actual peripheral
or board observation. A successful emulator run does not establish silicon
timing, analog behavior, retention or physical fault recovery. A finite event
history does not establish schedulability outside its stated arrival,
preemption, blocking, deadline and resource premises.

Source access also has a boundary. The held CSAPP and TLPI assignments now
have fresh bounded passage evidence and a claim comparison. The exact board
schematic and named complete server proofs remain source gaps where retrieval
failed. A bibliography or delegated proof citation cannot supply that evidence.
Published ISO editions remain separate from the inspected drafts and selected
compiler support probes.

The source-bound author evidence does not assess a learner, perform an external
review, or replace the original delayed transfer and physical-capture work.
