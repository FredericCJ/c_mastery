PASS / same FAIL / UNRESOLVED replay rule and reduced trigger:

Invariant-based repair; nearby case rejecting a symptom-only patch:

Debug/release/instrumented regression evidence and observer effects:

Guided, solo-seeded or genuinely withheld assessment? Who supplied the case? When was the oracle revealed?

Seven-day changed-capacity/failure/schedule task; result and reopened claims:

Distinguish stack-local buffer corruption from stack exhaustion. Distinguish fixed `.bss` placement/startup faults from overwriting a global and custom heap/stack collision. Distinguish unreachable leaks from retained reachable storage. Clearing one pointer does not repair aliases. Cancellation request does not establish quiescence. A sanitizer's silence establishes neither ownership nor absence of undefined behavior.
