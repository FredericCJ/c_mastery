# X-T38-002 technical analysis

Output bits for A5 are start0,1,0,1,0,0,1,0,1,stop1. N365 follows nearest-divisor selection; the fractional field moves under OVER8. Mutants reverse the first data bit or store N directly as BRR. The asymmetric01 and OVER8 cases reject them. The model intentionally drops application delivery on ORE although the hardware may retain the previous RDR byte; require the learner to explain that policy difference.
