## T51.03.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for owned mutexes and lock ordering.

Only the owner may release; recursion is refused under this contract. The checksum invariant may be temporarily broken only while protected. An acyclic global acquisition order avoids this class of lock cycle, not every possible wait deadlock.

Reject: A binary semaphore and owned mutex enforce the same misuse rules.
