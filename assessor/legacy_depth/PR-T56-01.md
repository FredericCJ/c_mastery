# PR-T56-01 independent technical assessment

Same-author technical analysis, excluded from learner package. Historical accepted evidence remains unchanged.

<a id="t56-01-a"></a>
## t56-01-a

Thread exit alone does not release the process address space while another live thread remains. Each TCB owns a saved frame and stack; process resources have a different lifetime.

Required remaining mechanism/evidence: Expanded cancellation/reference accounting and independent process/thread identity assessment remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Terminate the process while one thread runs remotely and prove no frame reuse until every CPU leaves.

A repeated output without derivation and scope is at most level1. A source/model check never substitutes for required guest or physical execution.

## t56-01-b

The bounded profile admits at most4 and rejects the fifth withEAGAIN. Join consumes one generation handle; the second join is invalid and cannot reclaim a new incarnation.

Required remaining mechanism/evidence: Expanded cancellation/reference accounting and independent process/thread identity assessment remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Exhaust the global32-thread pool across several processes without corrupting existing ready/wait lists.

A repeated output without derivation and scope is at most level1. A source/model check never substitutes for required guest or physical execution.
