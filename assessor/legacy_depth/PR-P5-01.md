## T24.08.b — Handle registration changes, descriptor reuse, and stale events.

Event identity must validate live registration/lifetime. Readiness is permission to attempt bounded progress, not a complete frame or ownership transfer. Edge-triggered draining needs a rearm/backpressure plan when the output queue fills.

Reject: A descriptor number is a permanent connection identity.

Discriminator: require the first divergent state/ownership edge/output or assumption, not only a final checksum. Check the actual producer and adapter revisions, the independence of the expected result, and whether the claimed environment was executed. integration.p5 is a synchronous cancellation/completion/discard anchor; it does not establish the concurrent Linux reader/decoder/worker or load/shutdown campaign.

## T25.02.c — Prefer reduced sharing before introducing fine-grained synchronization.

Immutable data still needs publication and lifetime, while private mutable state needs no sharing lock. Reduced sharing shrinks synchronization obligations but does not eliminate the queue/control-state protocol. The synchronous p5 anchor cannot prove the concurrent design.

Reject: Every field visible to two threads needs a separate fine-grained lock.

Discriminator: require the first divergent state/ownership edge/output or assumption, not only a final checksum.
