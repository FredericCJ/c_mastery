## t55-02-a

Successful interference can witness system progress while one caller starves. This is not a bounded wait-free guarantee; include allocation, reclamation and logging paths.

Required remaining mechanism/evidence: Standalone CAS counter, spurious-failure model and whole-call progress dossier remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Add a blocking allocator to an otherwise lock-free loop and revise the end-to-end classification.

A repeated output without derivation and scope is at most level1. A source/model check never substitutes for required guest or physical execution.
