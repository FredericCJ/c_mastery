## T51.09.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for counted task notifications.

The ordinary trace leaves1. At7+2 an all-or-error operation refuses and leaves7; a saturating model accepts only1 and leaves8. Both require distinct loss accounting. UINT_MAX arithmetic must be checked before addition.

Reject: Saturating a count silently is equivalent to reporting overflow.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.
