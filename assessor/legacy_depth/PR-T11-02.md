## T11.04.c — Decide whether to link, implement, or avoid each helper.

In ordinary zero-initialized single-numerator division, r<=processed_prefix<2^127 before each shift, so high carry is unreachable; its removal is equivalent. Streaming r<divisor can have a129th temporary bit and needs an appropriate representation. mi_divide returns0 on divisor0; fr_divmod128 refuses without changing outputs; emitted C division has no defined divisor0 semantics. Compare only equivalent nonzero domains when reporting overhead.

Reject superficial reasoning: A local helper is correct because it passes small integers, or avoiding a helper always costs less.

Discriminator: Before iteration k (0<=k<128), the processed numerator prefix is less than2^k and remainder is at most that prefix. Thus remainder<2^127 before every shift. Its top carry bit is always zero. Removing that redundant branch is an equivalent mutation for this domain; no honest test can reject it as incorrect. Streaming continuation admits arbitrary remainder r<divisor. With r=2^127+3,d=2^127+7 and bit1, (2r+1) divmod d is (1,2^127). A129th temporary carry is necessary unless another mathematically equivalent representation is used.

Delayed assessor check: Add remainder output and prove quotient*divisor+remainder in an independent unbounded mathematical oracle without target overflow.
