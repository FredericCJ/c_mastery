## t56-03-a

Each admitted first touch obtains a zero-filled frame before present mapping publication. Reservation is not allocation, and stale allocator contents must never become user-visible.

Required remaining mechanism/evidence: Expanded OOM/kernel-fault attribution and first-touch frame-ledger assessment remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Seed freed frames with nonzero synthetic data and independently verify all4096 bytes after first touch.

A repeated output without derivation and scope is at most level1. A source/model check never substitutes for required guest or physical execution.
