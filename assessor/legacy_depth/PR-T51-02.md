## T51.02.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for counting and binary semaphores.

After two successful acquisitions and three accepted posts the stored count is2; the fourth post is refused. Direct handoff transfers a token without storing a duplicate. Count plus outstanding ownership must match accepted production less consumption.

Reject: A post that wakes a waiter must also increment available count.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Delayed assessor check: Add a timed-out waiter immediately before a post and derive which destination owns the token.
