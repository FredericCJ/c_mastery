## t55-01-a

Producer payload precedes release head; consumer acquire head precedes read. Consumer read precedes release tail; producer acquire tail precedes overwrite. Own-index relaxed loads do not replace these cross-party edges.

Required remaining mechanism/evidence: Independent ordering model, multicore SPSC stress and measured sharing costs remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Batch records and prove the admitted batch publication/reuse boundaries without inserting a second writer.

A repeated output without derivation and scope is at most level1. A source/model check never substitutes for required guest or physical execution.
