# PR-T47-07 supplemental assessor analysis

Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

<a id="t4707a"></a>
## T47.07.a

The proof is conditional on the adapter. A failed premise invalidates the guarantee rather than becoming another admitted old-or-new schedule.

Expected relation: No reused redo bytes coexist with an authoritative old marker; a new valid marker follows durable after-images; acknowledged success recovers new state.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Allow a flush to lie and identify exactly which implication fails.

<a id="t4707b"></a>
## T47.07.b

An application-level retry needs its own deduplication/reconciliation contract. The storage error category must not be flattened into a Boolean failure.

Expected relation: Prepublication failures recover old state under the model; publication uncertainty may recover old or new; poisoned handles refuse ordinary use.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Expose the operation through a non-idempotent application service and add an operation identifier.

<a id="t4707c"></a>
## T47.07.c

Crash consistency is not merely open-success or structural validity. Application semantics and acknowledgment conditions are essential.

Expected relation: Acknowledged success requires new state; the mutant violates that stronger relation,while indeterminate response permits either complete map.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Delay the flush acknowledgment after persistence and test the caller's recovery path.
