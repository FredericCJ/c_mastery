## T51.06.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for bounded message queues with copied payloads.

The received bytes equal the accepted send snapshot, not subsequent sender changes. Payload ownership must remain unambiguous on timeout, full and receiver failure. The integer anchor alone cannot establish byte-length or output-capacity behavior.

Reject: Copying a pointer into a queue transfers its pointed-to storage safely.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

## T51.06.b — Implement a fixed-capacity queue with try/block/timed send and receive. Record message sequence IDs and lengths, then run multiple producers and consumers with explicit shutdown.

Failure preserves the queued head and caller output under this contract. The newly free slot admits at most one pending sender; closed queues reject new sends while draining already accepted bytes. Bound all offset/count arithmetic before copying.

## T51.06.c — Account for every accepted message exactly once and preserve per-producer order. Rejected sends preserve occupancy; normal close rejects new sends and drains queued messages, while explicit abort discards queued messages with a recorded cancellation count.
