## T34.06.b — Use restrict and alignment information only when contracts justify them.

Restrict is a semantic promise; false promises can invalidate correctness. Alignment of one pointer does not prove all iteration accesses aligned. Reports and disassembly show transformation, not correctness under illegal inputs.
