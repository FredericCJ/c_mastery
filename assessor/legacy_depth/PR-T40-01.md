## t40-01-a

The original suite illustrates seven responsibilities but does not establish ISO protocol-suite conformance. R terminates two L1/L2 links and forwards L3; it need not decode L5/L6/L7.

Remaining required work: Independent boundary reconstruction and lifetime adapter evidence remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Replace the L1 byte service with a framed radio service and identify which original L2 duties remain necessary.

An expected value without its derivation, first-divergence diagnosis and observation scope is at most level1. Physical criteria cannot pass from a simulation or prose plan.
