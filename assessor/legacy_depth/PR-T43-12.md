# PR-T43-12 supplemental assessor analysis

Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

<a id="t4313a"></a>
## T43.13.a

Architecture addresses do not prove selected-board behavior. A replacement timer changes the observation model and source requirements.

Expected relation: Unknown, absent, disabled and unowned are distinct results; no actual MMIO or setup change is claimed by the certificate.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Use a device timer with a different clock and state its own access and ownership prerequisites.

<a id="t4313b"></a>
## T43.13.b

A single frequency conversion assumes a stable clock. Piecewise clock evidence or a different elapsed-time observer is required when that assumption changes.

Expected relation: The bounded case gives64cycles and4000ns; a full-wrap-admitting interval is ambiguous; delta above the declared bound rejects.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Change clock frequency during the interval and derive what can still be inferred.

<a id="t4313c"></a>
## T43.13.c

Cycle count can change with wait states and interrupts even at a fixed clock. A functional emulator result cannot close those physical questions.

Expected relation: No silicon, energy or WCET claim is made without its corresponding observation; halt intervals invalidate simple wall-time conversion.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Transfer the kernel to a cached Cortex-M7 and identify new memory-system assumptions.
