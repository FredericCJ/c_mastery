## T53.09.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for tlb shootdown and page reclamation.

Other CPUs may retain old translations. Reuse must wait for every relevant participant's completed invalidation or a separately proved nonuse/quiescence path. A stale acknowledgement cannot satisfy the current generation. An IPI is not itself the invalidation.

Reject: A coherent PTE store instantly revokes every CPU's old translation.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Delayed assessor check: Add CPU entry into the address space concurrent with shootdown and derive membership synchronization.

## T53.09.b — Implement bounded shootdown requests, local invalidation and acknowledgments before reclaim. Begin with one page and two CPUs using the same address space.

Timeout does not authorize reuse. CR3 reload scope depends on PCID/global-page assumptions, requiring exact inspection before stronger claims.
