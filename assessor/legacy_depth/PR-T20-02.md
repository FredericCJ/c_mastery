## T20.05.a

The fixture's driver/BSP/HAL labels define this architecture only. Vendor naming differences require semantic mapping, not forced renaming.

Expected relation: Portable threshold decisions stay in logical units; board polarity changes at the declared wiring boundary; register sequencing follows the selected driver contract.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Support two boards with opposite polarity through one service binary and compare configuration ownership.

Expected relation: The comparison has equal functional/failure obligations and actual dependency evidence; missing target execution remains open.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Require concurrent use by two clients and reassess locking, initialization and state ownership.

## T20.05.c

A generic read-modify-write helper can violate register behavior. No physical register experiment is authorized or claimed by this source-level assessment.

Expected relation: The service sees only declared logical values/errors and keeps identical threshold behavior across equivalent adapters.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Introduce write-one-to-clear status and show why its semantics must remain in the driver even if exposed through a generic function.
