## T51.01.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for atomic wait enrollment and lost-wakeup prevention.

Publishing after an unprotected false check but before enrollment can strand the consumer. A protected recheck/enrollment closes that interval; a pending predicate and a wake event are different state. The model proves only its chosen serialization assumptions.

Reject: A wake is remembered even when nobody is enrolled.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Delayed assessor check: Replace one producer with an interrupt and identify the exact mask/lock boundary that must also protect enrollment.
