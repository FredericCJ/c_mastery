# PR-T43-02 supplemental assessor analysis

Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

<a id="t4303a"></a>
## T43.03.a

The usual expansion-only potential cannot cover an arbitrary shrink policy. A hysteresis threshold or revised charging scheme is needed, with the initial state stated.

Expected relation: The original successful-prefix bound holds only for its admitted operations; repeated failure adds separately bounded attempt cost and no ownership change.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Introduce deletion with shrink at half capacity and find a grow/shrink thrashing trace.

<a id="t4303b"></a>
## T43.03.b

A telescoping identity alone is not a bound. Nonnegative final potential and an accounted initial potential determine whether actual work is bounded by charged work.

Expected relation: Telescoping always includes Phi0; a negative initial potential requires an offset or a restricted reachable-state argument before deriving an upper bound.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Construct a piecewise potential supporting expansion and quarter-full shrinking.

<a id="t4303c"></a>
## T43.03.c

A handle move can preserve constant per-element cost if ownership rules permit it; ISO C does not enforce that logical move or prevent duplicate destruction.

Expected relation: The claim is deterministic over the declared sequence but depends on copy weights; worst single growth and allocator latency remain separate.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Use move-only logical ownership with a constant-size handle and compare rollback requirements.
