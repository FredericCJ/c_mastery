## T51.07.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for mailboxes and synchronous rendezvous.

Mailbox send may finish on buffering; rendezvous finishes on transfer, still before processing. Cancellation before transfer can prevent it; after transfer cannot retract the receiver's value. A bounded protocol must expose that distinction.

Reject: A successful send always means the receiver processed the message.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Delayed assessor check: Add a reply and distinguish delivery completion from end-to-end request completion.
