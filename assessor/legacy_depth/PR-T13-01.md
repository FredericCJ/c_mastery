# PR-T13-01 additive assessor analysis

Withheld from learner distribution. Author analysis only; retained historical evidence is not relabeled as fresh acceptance.

<a id="t1301a"></a>

## T13.01.a

Outputs do not determine effects. A transitive contract cannot be stronger than an unresolved reachable callback contract; distinguish unknown from prohibited and observed-zero.

Withheld discriminator: Replace the callback with one that allocates and blocks while returning the same value. Require the changed invariant or interface argument and distinguish the independent task from reproducing the public case.

<a id="t1301b"></a>

## T13.01.b

Moving cost changes the accounting boundary, not its existence. The resource contract must state inclusion of startup and retained state.

Withheld discriminator: Move cache creation to process startup and revisit whether the component or deployment owns its cost. Require the changed invariant or interface argument and distinguish the independent task from reproducing the public case.

## T13.01.c

Do not sum unrelated exclusive paths or subtract frames based on guessed optimization. The proof names simultaneous lifetimes and the compiler/ABI model.

Withheld discriminator: Tail-call elimination removes one frame in a specific build; compare the conditional artifact observation with the conservative source model. Require the changed invariant or interface argument and distinguish the independent task from reproducing the public case.
