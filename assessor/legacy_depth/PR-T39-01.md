# PR-T39-01 independent technical assessment

Author-authored assessment only. Keep these discriminators outside learner packages. Historical accepted records are unchanged.

<a id="t39-01-a"></a>
## t39-01-a

PDU sizes are 20, 40 and 54. These illustrative lengths do not establish that the bytes form valid UDP, IP or Ethernet packets.

Remaining required work: Bounded C wrapper/interface checker and independent delayed assessment remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Replace a frame service with a byte stream and derive the framing and ownership adapter before claiming compatibility.

An expected value without its derivation, first-divergence diagnosis and observation scope is at most level1. Physical criteria cannot pass from a simulation or prose plan.
