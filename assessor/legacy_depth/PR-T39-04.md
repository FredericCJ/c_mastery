## t39-05-a

RMII reference and MDC are different clocks; selected divide 102 gives about 1.647 MHz MDC and PHY0/register2 read-start word 0x91.

Remaining required work: Actual bounded C transaction/identity/status adapter and physical clock/link observations remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Change HCLK to an excluded 10 MHz and reject rather than inventing a legal divider from another target.
