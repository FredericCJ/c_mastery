# PR-T48-04 supplemental assessor analysis

Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

<a id="t4804a"></a>
## T48.04.a

Allocator correctness depends on the reservation map and legal transitions. Zero as failure must not accidentally reserve a valid frame under a changed address policy.

Expected relation: Exactly256 distinct frames emerge initially; conservation and exclusive membership hold after every operation.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Introduce a reserved hole and derive the exact returned set, not just a smaller total.

<a id="t4804b"></a>
## T48.04.b

The retained loader may choose fatal admission failure; that does not satisfy a newly claimed recoverable operation. Separate those policies explicitly.

Expected relation: Each rejected request leaves prior mappings and free count intact; success exposes only zeroed pages with intended permissions.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Allocate a range crossing an existing mapping and refuse without damaging it.

<a id="t4804c"></a>
## T48.04.c

Reclamation requires quiescence of all users. Single-core scheduling establishes only the stated local handoff; SMP TLB/users require later mechanisms.

Expected relation: No current address-space or execution-stack storage is reused; accounting names intentionally static retained storage.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Design dynamic process admission and derive the extra teardown and slot-generation obligations.
