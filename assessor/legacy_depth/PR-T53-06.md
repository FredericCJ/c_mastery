## T53.06.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for per-cpu ready queues.

The stages have different architectures; a later global-queue pass cannot evidence a per-CPU design. For each transition, runnable ownership must be unique and simultaneous execution forbidden. Trace omission cannot be treated as proof of absence.

Reject: Every scheduler in the retained SMP family implements per-CPU queues.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Delayed assessor check: Add a task moving between queues and extend the state model with an explicit transfer state.
