# PR-T43-08 supplemental assessor analysis

Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

<a id="t4309a"></a>
## T43.09.a

A host clock and a modular counter need different admissibility proofs. The observed difference cannot itself prove the interval bound.

Expected relation: Equal reads are valid; reversed/overflowed intervals reject; resolution, accuracy and bracket cost remain distinct.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Use a wrapping hardware counter with an independently justified less-than-one-wrap bound.

<a id="t4309b"></a>
## T43.09.b

A deterministic order can confound treatment and drift. Experimental design and raw observations are part of the evidence, not optional presentation detail.

Expected relation: Both statistics are reported under their own definitions; pair identities, setup exclusions and overhead sensitivity remain reproducible.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Introduce a drift trend and compare blocked versus alternating trial order.

<a id="t4309c"></a>
## T43.09.c

Removing censored failures biases latency evidence. A research conclusion can remain inconclusive when the available experiment cannot establish the required guarantee.

Expected relation: Report sample size, misses and sensitivity; no finite maximum is relabeled a universal guarantee.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Introduce censored timeout trials and derive a report that cannot hide them as missing data.
