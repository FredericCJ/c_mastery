## T52.08.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for tick rollover, phase and tickless timing.

Modular subtraction orders only a declared window shorter than half the modulus; exactly half is ambiguous. Equality is due. An infinite wait is separate state, preventing accidental collision with a real tick value.

Reject: Unsigned subtraction orders arbitrary dates correctly.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Reject: Rounding up to3 ticks guarantees15ms elapsed from any call instant.
