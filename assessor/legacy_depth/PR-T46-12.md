## T46.12.a

Reproducibility and independent mastery are separate. Author-run reference campaigns do not establish a learner's ability.

Expected relation: Each boundary has its exact artifact and scope; mismatched provenance rejects; finite tests remain bounded evidence.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Have a second reader reproduce a selected chain from a clean learner export.

## T46.12.b

Visible mutants are guided regression controls. An unrevealed defect with independently established acceptance is needed for independent diagnosis assessment.

Expected relation: The evidence shows the specific violated property; no privilege or arbitrary-code-execution claim follows from a crash; untested mutations remain open.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Combine an accepted verifier case with a host-lifetime defect to test boundary composition.

Expected relation: The new resource/lifetime/failure proof and tests address the changed mechanism; the assessment records whether the variant was truly withheld.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Compose the VM service with an asynchronous store adapter and specify continuation ownership.
