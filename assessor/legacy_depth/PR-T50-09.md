Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

## T50.09.a

The runtime must not free or overwrite the stack containing its current cleanup code. Logical and execution ownership compose.

Expected relation: Reclamation requires both logical no-users and actual switch-away; one joiner consumes one result.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Allow detached tasks and specify who performs final reap.

<a id="t5009b"></a>
## T50.09.b

The retained model refuses certain owned resources on exit. A new robust policy needs explicit invariant repair before reuse.

Expected relation: Exactly one permitted result delivery occurs; failed joins preserve state; owned-resource exit refusal remains explicit.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Add a supervisor policy for faulted resource owners and keep poison distinct from normal release.

## T50.09.c

The delayed assessment should combine identity and reclamation under changed storage placement, not merely change pool capacity.

Expected relation: Each stack has a complete lifetime ledger and retired generations do not wrap into validity.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Move task results into caller-owned storage and derive its required lifetime.
