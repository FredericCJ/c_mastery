## T53.04.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for local apic timers and interprocessor interrupts.

ICR status concerns delivery machinery; EOI completes interrupt-controller service; application completion requires a published result for the expected generation and CPU. Duplicate or stale acknowledgements cannot satisfy missing participants.

Reject: ICR idle or EOI proves the requested work has finished.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Reject: The APIC timer always runs at a fixed known frequency in every power state.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.
