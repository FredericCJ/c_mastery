Expected relation: The minimal library/client build succeeds without tests,examples or documentation tools; accidental private dependencies are detected by the consumer.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Move a private generated header to the build tree and verify it cannot leak into the installed interface.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Build both adapters into one test process while keeping their separate contexts and contracts.
