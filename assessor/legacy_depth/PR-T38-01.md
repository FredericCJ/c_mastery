# PR-T38-01 independent technical assessment

Author-authored assessment only. Keep these discriminators outside learner packages. Historical accepted records are unchanged.

<a id="t38-01-a"></a>
## t38-01-a

USART frames bits, transceiver changes electrical levels, CPU/MMIO transfers payload; neither Cortex-M4 nor ISO C specifies the board route.

Remaining required work: Actual pin/RCC/DMA reachability adapter and physical timing remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Replace the USART with SPI-to-external-CAN and relocate framing, arbitration and electrical responsibilities.

An expected value without its derivation, first-divergence diagnosis and observation scope is at most level1. Physical criteria cannot pass from a simulation or prose plan.
