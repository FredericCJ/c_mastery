## T51.11.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for once initialization and failure policy.

Permanent failure permits one failed attempt and stable failure for B/C; retry policy returns to an initializable state. CMX_RETRY marks a role/result requiring re-entry, while bridge MB_RETRY is CAS contention. These names are not interchangeable.

Reject: CMX_RETRY proves the historical permanent-failure contract passed.

A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.
