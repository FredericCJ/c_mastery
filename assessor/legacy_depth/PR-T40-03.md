# PR-T40-03 independent technical assessment

Author-authored assessment only. Keep these discriminators outside learner packages. Historical accepted records are unchanged.

<a id="t40-03-a"></a>
## t40-03-a

CRC covers unescaped header/payload, then body including CRC is escaped. Body<=47 and worst wire<=96; CRC check123456789 is29B1. CRC over escaped bytes is a different wire contract.

Remaining required work: Grant/local-destination adapter, exhaustive recovery/split evidence and independent transfer remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Change the escaping convention in a separately versioned protocol and reconstruct its independent polynomial/length oracle.

An expected value without its derivation, first-divergence diagnosis and observation scope is at most level1. Physical criteria cannot pass from a simulation or prose plan.

<a id="t40-03-b"></a>
## t40-03-b

Exactly the final valid candidate publishes; bounded DISCARD state recovers at delimiter and no rejected candidate mutates upper output. The retained byte decoder has no local/grant parameters, so its caller must enforce those before delivery.

Remaining required work: Grant/local-destination adapter, exhaustive recovery/split evidence and independent transfer remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Coalesce two valid frames around a UART error and derive which candidate is invalidated without discarding an unrelated completed one.

An expected value without its derivation, first-divergence diagnosis and observation scope is at most level1. Physical criteria cannot pass from a simulation or prose plan.

<a id="t40-03-c"></a>
## t40-03-c

Integrity alone does not authorize local delivery; neither frame may reach upper state. Successful CRC does not authenticate a sender or establish routed/end-to-end delivery.

Remaining required work: Grant/local-destination adapter, exhaustive recovery/split evidence and independent transfer remain required.

Transfer discriminator: Construct a deliberate valid-CRC modified payload in the local teaching program and explain why adding more CRC bits still does not supply authentication.

An expected value without its derivation, first-divergence diagnosis and observation scope is at most level1. Physical criteria cannot pass from a simulation or prose plan.
