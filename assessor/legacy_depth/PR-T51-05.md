## T51.05.a — Derive and defend the state, ownership and ordering contract for condition variables and mesa-style predicates.

The signaled waiter is eligible to reacquire, not entitled to the item. It can find the predicate false and wait again. Release/enrollment must be atomic relative to the predicate updater; successful wait returns owning the associated mutex.

Reject: Signal transfers the mutex and guarantees the predicate on return.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.

Delayed assessor check: Replace signal with broadcast for three consumers and one item; derive all wake/recheck outcomes.

## T51.05.b — Implement condition wait/signal/broadcast on the common wait protocol. Build a bounded producer-consumer object whose predicate is protected by the same mutex.

Under the required reacquire contract, deadline expiration selects the result but return can wait longer for the mutex. Cancellation/close cannot bypass ownership restoration. The model's pending/result fields must distinguish those phases.

Reject: A timed wait must return immediately when its deadline expires.

Assessor discriminator: require the first state, result, ownership edge or schedule event that differs under the rejected explanation. A generic stress checksum or missing sanitizer finding is not a substitute for that witness. Confirm the claim stays inside the declared execution and source scope.
