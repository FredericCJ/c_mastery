Assessor-only; author reasoning, not independent or learner evidence.

## T49.07.a

Open-file-description sharing, descriptor flags and process ownership are different contracts. Lifetime is not represented by the integer alone.

Expected relation: The fixture offsets are a5,b5,c4; stale numeric references can target a different later object.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Add a descriptor transfer and extend the reference ledger before using it.

## T49.07.b

A leak can appear as a liveness failure. Arbitrary sleeps do not make the required close ordering deterministic.

Expected relation: Shared offsets and independent opens match prediction; failure cleanup leaves no owned descriptor or child behind.

Withheld transfer discriminator: Use close-on-exec at creation and verify the executed child cannot inherit the private endpoint.

Expected relation: Linux-specific analysis matches inspected close documentation; POSIX2008/2024 claims are distinguished from direct standard inspection and not silently generalized.
