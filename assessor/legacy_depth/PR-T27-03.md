Assessor only. Authored technical arguments do not assert a learner attempt, independent human review or fresh execution of the complete project. The original implementation contract remains binding.

## T27.05.a — Copy initialized sections from load storage to execution storage.

Copy source and destination roles come from the linker/image contract; count arithmetic and nonoverlap must be established before access.

Reject: The load address and execution address of initialized data are always equal.

Evidence discriminator: locate the first task-specific state, return value, ownership transition or artifact that would differ under the rejected explanation. Require that observation or a complete finite derivation; reject a generic checksum, sanitizer absence or source heading as a substitute.

Delayed assessor check: Place one initialized object in a second RAM bank with a distinct load image and derive the new copy table.

## T27.05.b — Clear intended zero-initialized regions without corrupting retained data.

Zero initialization applies to selected objects; retained/no-init regions have an independent validity protocol and must not be silently erased.
