## T24.01.a — Distinguish generation, pending state, masking, and delivery.

Pending membership is not a count; standard-signal coalescing prevents inferring two application events. Generation, eligibility and delivery are distinct states.

Reject: Pending standard signals form an unlimited counted queue.

Evidence discriminator: locate the first task-specific state, return value, ownership transition or artifact that would differ under the rejected explanation. Require that observation or a complete finite derivation; reject a generic checksum, sanitizer absence or source heading as a substitute.

Delayed assessor check: Replace the standard signal with a bounded real-time-signal experiment and record queue-capacity/failure semantics.

## T24.02.c — Eliminate lost-wakeup windows during shutdown and child handling.

Notifications indicate work may exist; draining/rechecking state closes coalescing and arrival windows. A child result remains owned until reaped.

Reject: One SIGCHLD notification corresponds to exactly one reap.

Evidence discriminator: locate the first task-specific state, return value, ownership transition or artifact that would differ under the rejected explanation. Require that observation or a complete finite derivation; reject a generic checksum, sanitizer absence or source heading as a substitute.
