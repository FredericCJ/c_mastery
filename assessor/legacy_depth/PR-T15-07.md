## T15.10.a

Span boundaries and empty-field semantics are part of the interface. A parser must not silently change them based on string-library conventions.

Withheld discriminator: Change to drop-empty policy and identify which round-trip/information properties are lost. Require the changed invariant or interface argument and distinguish the independent task from reproducing the public case.
