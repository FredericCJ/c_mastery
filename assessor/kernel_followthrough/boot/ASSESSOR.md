# Boot boundary assessment

Reveal only after the learner freezes a prediction and reproducer. The author
campaign is preparation, not blind assessment.

For T48.01.a, require a transition ledger naming the owner of each assumption.
The decisive observations are retained EAX/EBX at `_start`, CR0/CR3/CR4/EFER,
CS64 after the far transfer, all2048 page entries, and entry RSP. A count alone
cannot establish correct mode state. For the relocated variant, require the
new symbol addresses and reservation set, including the boot-information span
supplied by this particular GRUB run. Do not assume it relocates by the same
constant as the ELF.

For T48.01.b, inspect the exact ELF and ISO identities; no scheduler or later
provider may be silently linked into the early milestone. Distinguish the
header's file offset from its physical address. The reordered case must still
be discoverable, and the undefined-symbol set must be empty. The probe's byte
and port routines are owned target code; GRUB/QEMU/linker are author tools.

For T48.01.c, the missing-info control must show nonzero EBX at GRUB entry but
zero RSI/info at C entry. The omitted far-transfer control must retain CS32
where the repaired image has CS64. A blank serial log alone is not a diagnosis. Clang’s wrong-mode control even
emits a corrupted partial C marker; that does not establish a legal C entry.
The alignment control reaches the explicit `STACK_ALIGNMENT` refusal. Require
the matching repair image and the first differing state, not only a pass marker.

After at least seven days, give an unrevealed load-address/section-order/stack
layout change. Vary one premise first; request a prediction before building.
Score the original rubric against the learner's own image, source map and
causal explanation. Record assessor disclosure, elapsed interval and help used.
Independent review, delayed learner execution and physical observations remain
unadministered in the author receipt.
