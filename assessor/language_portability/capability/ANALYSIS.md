## T05.04.a ordinary and delayed

The output structure receives a value copy while the literal is alive. Returning
`&(struct lp_value){...}` from a function instead makes the caller's access occur after
the automatic object's lifetime. That counterexample requires explanation,
not an attempted output prediction. Restricted source/destination spans must
be disjoint when destination writes modify objects also accessed through source.
The preflight scan validates every product before the second scan publishes;
source `[1,INT_MAX/2,INT_MAX]` must leave `[91,92,93]` intact. A successful
ordinary example alone will not catch the partial-publication mutant.
`LP_TRACE` has one call expression. Its two different incremented counters
avoid a conflicting same-scalar argument expression; their relative order is
unspecified and irrelevant. `_Generic` controls are not evaluated. Full credit
requires the learner to state these distinct sequencing premises.

## T05.04.b ordinary and delayed

## T05.04.c ordinary and delayed

The C90 reference prints only empty and three `long` values. `intmax_t` can
