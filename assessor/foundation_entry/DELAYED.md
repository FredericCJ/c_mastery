# Seven-day independent transfer package

Record a source-bound study event, wait at least seven days, then issue the six
unchanged `-DELAYED` AX tasks from PR-T01-01 without the reference source or this
analysis. Save the assigned contract before implementation. This authored package
and its reference execution do not establish that any learner has done so.

1. T01.01.a: require the explicit seventeen-record stack, base activation and
   partial failure trace. Supply `[MIN,2,-2]` and ask which unwind can fail before
   allowing execution. Discriminate an unbounded C recursion hidden behind an API.
2. T01.01.b: remove the sixteen-element bound and support both two-pass and
   allocated staging under their different overlap contracts. Inject acquisition
   failure and checked-add failure after acquisition. Require exact ownership and
   an aliasing counterexample; success only at n<=16 is insufficient.
3. T01.01.c: derive total empty maximum selection and its invariant independently.
   Include empty, singleton MIN and a negative-only range; zero initialization
   must be rejected by the explanation as well as tests.
4. T01.02.a: derive the three-octet most-significant-first contract. Withhold the
   byte sequence for `0x800001`, and ask for the reasoning under an opposite-endian
   host. Expected `[80,00,01]`; reject capacity2 without any publication.
5. T01.02.b: given required mask0x14 and forbidden mask0x28, derive admission,
   then diagnose contradictory required/forbidden mask0x04. A correct isolated
   predicate with a mistaken combined permission policy is not independent transfer.
6. T01.02.c: supply saturating addition as a policy requirement; require a
   reassociation counterexample and comparison with signed/unsigned C rules.
   Changing the constants of a copied example without deriving the grouping
   difference does not satisfy level3.

Assess each with the public row-specific rubric and private ANALYSIS. Retain
predictions, source, compiler commands, minimized failed candidates, semantic
