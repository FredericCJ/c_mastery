# P4 withheld assessment protocol

Release after the retained minimum seven days and without reference sources,
private tests or prior worked answers. Record actual attempt date, source hashes,
assessor identity and unchanged prerequisite evidence. No attempt is fabricated.

For T27.08.b relocate DMA storage to another SRAM window, then offer CCM as an
incorrect candidate. Require reset/vector/stack/helper evidence and a rejection
before enabling DMA. For T28.05.c add half-duplex direction with TC and qualified
turnaround; inject TXE without TC, wrapped ticks and a stalled acknowledgement.
For T29.06.b introduce a second producer. Require an explicit lost reservation
trace and a complete redesign argument; separate SPSC queues are valid if source
identity and ordering limitations are stated.

For T29.06.c reduce physical ring4 to2 with identical offered events. Require
accepted/dropped/missing/failed counts and latency distributions, preserving raw
captures. Synthetic predictions are acceptable only in their declared author
model tier when a board is absent. For T29.09.b use a split64 counter on32-bit
hardware; stop at every rollover store and at sequence exhaustion. Require a
bounded failure result and a progress argument under preemption.

For T30.05.b add the private C3/T20/J0 workload while retaining the original
C12/T50/J5 interrupt. Require a revised interference recurrence and independent
schedule, plus rejection of the old74us bound. For T30.05.c change O0→O3 or clock
configuration. Require an invalidation table for image, cycle conversion, UART
baud, instrumentation and prior distributions; new physical measurements remain
pending until observed. For T31.10.c fail abort acknowledgement repeatedly;
require retained-resource shutdown, first-fault preservation and no stale publish.

Score each retained rubric separately. A correct number with no model, copied
reference, one nominal pass, or a synthetic result labeled physical is inadequate.
Independent mastery requires reconstruction, counterexample, a valid alternative
or changed-condition repair, and precise evidence limits. The author campaign
executes these variants, not an independent learner attempt.
