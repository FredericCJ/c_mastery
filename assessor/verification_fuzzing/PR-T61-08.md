# Assessor analysis: PR-T61-08

A tool reporting success is one item in a proof argument. Missing unwinding assertions or overrestrictive assumptions can make success much weaker than the claim. Verification coverage and semantic correctness are distinct.

Bind the claim to source, compiler/solver configuration, assumptions, model bound and checked properties. Explain why local self-reported flags cannot establish reviewer independence or tool honesty.

The reference implementation is in `core.c`; inspect it together with the public test. The named fault is: A receipt omits complete unwinding but labels a loop universally safe. Reproduce the missing iteration counterexample and narrow the claim before attempting a genuine inductive proof.

Require the learner to derive 0 from the fixture inputs independently. Separate proof scope, execution observations, reviewer status and learner assessment.

Transfer answer obligations: Audit a new allocator or parser without the worked harness; choose proof, fuzzing and differential methods based on mechanisms and defend residual gaps after seven days.

Actual solver version/commands/results, reachable assumption witnesses, complete-unwinding evidence and bounded fuzz campaign logs remain distinct requirements; missing tools do not become successful proof.
