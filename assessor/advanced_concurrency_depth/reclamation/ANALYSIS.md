## T26.04.a: publication and histories

The link CAS is enqueue's linearization point. Tail is a helpable hint; the forced `publication` case pauses the actual producer after node0.next becomes1, before Tail changes. The consumer advances Tail then Head and returns11. The producer's later Tail CAS may fail harmlessly. Head CAS linearizes a successful dequeue; the validated empty-link observation linearizes RETRY. A bounded allocation-scan failure is permissive admission failure, not a claim of logical FIFO fullness. The historical source pseudocode assumes a type-preserving allocator; ordinary free would invalidate its unprotected access premise.

The new queue uses the retained CMX mechanism with an explicit registration lease, two-word payload, selectable participant count and reduced generation limit. The frozen CMX producer is still actually compiled in the reservation comparison and the retained queue tests. `history_tests.c` records real invocations/responses from four participants, not a generated list labeled as a run. The frozen AH checker emits a witness; this test replays it, and a separate permutation oracle checks each actual trace. Two successful enqueues fit capacity4 without allocation rejection, so these histories do not reinterpret FULL as logical-full. A deliberately reversed sequential removal fails FIFO. Finite histories and stress cover only their recorded executions.

## T26.04.b: why cancellation is not disposal

CMX's reservation prefix deliberately returns RETRY until A publishes even if B is ready. AR names that state PENDING; calling it EMPTY would assert a stronger abstract object. AR's ticket-tagged cell prevents a delayed cancel CAS from affecting a later occupant. Producer finish is the sole publication authority. Requesting cancellation does not end its access: REQUESTED remains owned until finish changes it to CANCELLED after the last ordinary write. The forced worker is paused after writing, the request records successfully, and a consumer must still return PENDING. After acknowledgement it skips A and returns B. The cancel mutant exposes premature consumption through a harmless state assertion; it does not execute a native overlapping payload write.

Consumers CAS the head cell to CONSUMING before reading; they publish FREE and then Tail after the copy. A second consumer sees PENDING during this interval. A producer may reuse a slot only after observing its advanced Tail. Thus ordinary payload accesses have an SC publication/claim/reuse chain. A thread stopped in RESERVED or CONSUMING can block FIFO service, so this is an ownership-correct blocking-prefix design, not a lock-free queue. Ticket exhaustion is explicit; resetting counters is allowed only by destroying/reinitializing a quiescent object with no outstanding ticket.

## T26.04.c: participant assumptions

Two SPSC producers can both load head0, write different ordinary payloads to slot0, and store head1; one value is lost and the ordinary writes are unordered. Total atomic ordering cannot grant unique slot ownership. Two consumers can both return slot0 and grant duplicate reuse rights. The frozen SPSC integer countermodels expose these traces without executing undefined native races. AP repairs allocation with CAS and queue reachability with helpable link/head CAS, then adds protection before ordinary payload access. Exactly-once 1024/2048-value stress and real-time witness checks are distinct evidence, neither a universal linearizability proof nor a scheduler fairness proof.

## T26.06.a: complete protection and the justified weakening

The safety condition is continuous protection beginning while the node is still reachable. A reader loads the shared index, publishes a hazard and rereads the shared index. If removal/reuse occurred in the gap, it retries before accessing that incarnation. Head remains protected while its next link is loaded; after next protection, Head is checked again before reading successor payload. If Head has moved, next may already have changed lifetime, so no payload read is permitted. Validated current Head prevents its successor from being reclaimed without Head changing. A successful pop retires the old dummy only after Head CAS.

All protection publication, validation, root removal and scan operations remain SC. For the usual both-initial counterexample, SC would require publish-hazard < validate-old-root < remove-root < scan-empty-hazard < publish-hazard, an impossible cycle. Release/acquire loads that read initial values establish no synchronizes-with edge, so that replacement is not justified. The finite model has58states/85edges without bad reuse; skipped validation49/71 and ignored hazard44/66 each have an explicit bad state. This is a small falsification model, not a full C model checker proof.

The permitted delayed weakening is narrower: only the initial allocation candidate's state load becomes relaxed. Its result is advisory. Failure never grants access; success still requires an SC read-modify-write comparing the entire FREE incarnation. This RMW reads the immediately preceding modification and acquires the release that made storage free. All payload writes are sequenced after that claim; the protecting/collecting protocol and publication remain unchanged. A stale preliminary read can only lose admission or fail the full-state CAS, and conservative FULL was already permitted. This argument does not authorize weakening a retirement snapshot or hazard scan. Both compiled variants must preserve the original tests/history corpus.

A collector records the full retired generation, scans hazards, then CASes that exact state to the next FREE generation. The forced `collector` schedule pauses at retiredstate2; another collector releases it, it is reused and retired asstate6. The old CAS must fail; an untagged store incorrectly writesstate4 and exposes a later live/retired lifetime to reuse. Saturation prevents eventual state-tag ABA: limits1/3 admit63/127 total values and then leave31 permanently retired nodes plus one dummy. The maximum bound is finite, not an uptime guarantee.

## T26.06.b: bounds and pressure

Two hazards per P participants directly protect at most2P distinct nodes. A full32-node scan performs at most64P hazard loads plus up to32 state-CAS attempts. The fixture reserves one participant as the actor and uses the other P−1 participants to retain2(P−1) distinct retired nodes:6 atP4,14 atP8. With one dummy, remaining queue admission is25/17. Clearing one valid hazard releases one node and permits one enqueue. These figures are exact fixture results, not maxima of all delayed reclamation: unscanned retirements, private allocations and generation-exhausted nodes are additional storage consumers. Thread progress may continue with FULL while useful service is unavailable. Raising P without storage therefore reduces this pressure margin before any change to the fixed abstract FIFO operations.

## T26.06.c: registration and departure

A live lease is exclusive and protects a stable participant identity. On orderly exit, the owner finishes every access then clears hazards and changes the registration generation. A joined successor can do the same only after joining proves the thread cannot resume; a timeout cannot. The actual departure fixture leaves a hazard installed in a terminated thread, joins, observes blocked collection, then transfers cleanup and recovers the node. A paused validated reader remains protected and cannot be cleared by another participant. Registration reuse advances the generation, so stale APIs reject before clearing new hazards. Saturation retires the registration slot. None of this prevents a malicious caller from forging bits or directly writing public representation; those are precondition violations, not C type-system guarantees.
