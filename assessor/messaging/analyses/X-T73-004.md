A channel adapter may safely enqueue on the actor executor but direct foreign-thread enqueue needs synchronization.

## Delayed case X-T73-004-DELAYED

Messages become owned buffers and handlers may enqueue to themselves: implement deterministic destruction of abandoned messages without recursive dispatch. Delayed assessment receives a late reply addressed to a previous incarnation after restart; route it to an explicit dead-letter sink and prevent delivery to the replacement actor.

Require the revised contract, minimal counterexample, implementation/trace and cost evidence before feedback. Assess the invariant under the new assumptions, not just output similarity. The hidden reply retains generation1 while the replacement uses generation2 at the same storage address. A pointer-only check incorrectly accepts it. The dead-letter adapter must retain payload ownership and release it once even if the sink fails.
