# X-T73-003 assessor technical analysis

Copy receiver/context to local variables, publish DONE, call once and return without reading the operation again. The inline-completion path invalidates the common assumption that start can inspect its object after dispatching the backend. The serialized core does not solve concurrent duplicate completion; the backend must retain storage and arbitrate a winner before terminal release.

## Assessment boundaries

The assessor implementation is messaging.c in this separate directory tree. Review D1–D4 individually against the metadata rubric. The deliberate SC_MUTANT values 3 and 13 targets the operations anchor; require its named semantic assertion, not a compiler failure, timeout or unrelated crash. Additional independent tests remain mandatory.

## Delayed case X-T73-003-DELAYED

Allow an operation to complete inline during start and let the receiver destroy its enclosing object. Delayed assessment connects a chain of two operations; ensure the successor starts only after ownership transfers and no predecessor field is accessed after its receiver may have destroyed it.

Require the revised contract, minimal counterexample, implementation/trace and cost evidence before feedback. Assess the invariant under the new assumptions, not just output similarity. Have the successor complete inline as well and free the entire chain. The final source inspection must show no access through either old operation pointer after the terminal callback. A callback-count assertion alone misses that lifetime violation.

## Limits

This analysis records the technical solution and expected reasoning, not learner performance or a non-author review. Public-source comparisons are restricted to the registered inspected passages. Tests, sanitizers and bounded models do not prove arbitrary lifetimes, interleavings or environments correct.
