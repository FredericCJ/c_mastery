Keep the exact recorded reads-from graph; these outcomes are defined failures of
the atomic protocol, not executions of non-atomic data races.

The two proof directions are distinct: acquire of the completed even publication
orders earlier payload stores before reads; observing a new payload synchronizes
its release with the trailing acquire fence, ordering that writer's prior odd
sequence before the final sequence load. Atomic coherence then excludes the old
even result. Ordinary payloads would invalidate the argument before validation.
The model checks only the finite client under RC11/LLVM, not arbitrary C17 clients
or the inaccessible published C17 text. Native10,000-publication stress, counter

## Retained pointer lifetime successor

A retained snapshot is acquired under the root's mutex, before the writer can
remove its root reference. The reader then owns an independent count and may
pause while the writer replaces and drops the old root. The actual thread test
reads the former payload5 after replacement with9 and observes its destructor
only when the paused reader releases. Another retained snapshot survives root
control destruction. Sequence validation alone cannot do this: the local rejected
design demonstrates the root destructor at release without evaluating a dangling
pointer. A mutation omitting retain is rejected by that destructor boundary
before the paused reader resumes. A second mutation admits exhausted publication
counters and is rejected before ownership can be silently consumed.

The new contract deliberately changes the scalar mechanism: immutable payloads
and mutex-protected reference acquisition replace speculative raw-pointer reads.
The counter is publication metadata, not its lifetime mechanism. A learner must
account for the mutex and atomic reference traffic, blocked progress, allocator
context lifetime and potentially unbounded retention by stalled clients. No
reader may mutate shared payload through its exposed void pointer; ISO C does not
enforce that semantic restriction. Keep the RC11 scalar-order evidence distinct
from these native pointer-lifetime observations and from unseen learner transfer.
